    Jet Pump System Repair in Oregon City, OR

    Jet pumps, usually found above ground, use an ejector assembly to draw water from the well. Typical problems include air leaks in the suction line, clogged nozzles, or a failing motor. These issues can lead to intermittent water flow or a pump that runs continuously without building pressure. JFL Plumbing can reliably troubleshoot and repair these components, ensuring your jet pump system in Oregon City operates at peak efficiency. We also check for proper priming, which is crucial for jet pump function.

    Constant Pressure System Repair in Oregon City, OR

    Variable speed systems like the Grundfos SQE and Franklin SubDrive keep pressure steady by adjusting motor speed. When they fault out, the drive typically shows a code that guides diagnosis.     Pressure Switch Replacement in Oregon City

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, clogged sensing tubes and insect damage are frequent causes of failure. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank. This is one of the most budget-friendly well repairs.
