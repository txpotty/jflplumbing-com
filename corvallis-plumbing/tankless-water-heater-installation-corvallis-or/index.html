    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Corvallis, OR

    Calcium scale is the most common cause of tankless heater failure. Water in western Oregon is generally soft, while the drier eastern side tends to have harder, more mineral-rich groundwater. Scale coats the heat exchanger, reducing efficiency and triggering error codes. We install isolation valves so the unit can be flushed with a vinegar or descaling solution once a year. A scale inhibitor cartridge or whole-home softener provides added protection.
