    Well Pressure Tank Repair in Bend

    The pressure tank works with your well pump to maintain consistent water pressure and reduce pump cycling. Issues like a waterlogged tank, sudden pressure drops, or continuous pump operation often point to a faulty pressure tank. We inspect the air bladder, pressure switch, and tank connections to identify the problem. In Bend, we repair or replace tanks to optimize your well system's performance and extend pump life.

    Jet Pump System Repair in Bend, OR

    Jet pumps, usually found above ground, use an ejector assembly to draw water from the well. Common problems include air leaks in the suction line, clogged nozzles, or a failing motor. These issues can lead to intermittent water flow or a pump that runs continuously without building pressure. JFL Plumbing can effectively troubleshoot and repair these components, ensuring your jet pump system in Bend operates at peak efficiency. We also check for proper priming, which is crucial for jet pump function.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Bend

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, clogged sensing tubes and insect damage are common causes of failure. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank. This is one of the most affordable well repairs.
