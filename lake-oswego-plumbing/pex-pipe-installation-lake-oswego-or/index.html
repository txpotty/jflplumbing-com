    Full Home PEX Repiping in Lake Oswego

    If your home has old, corroded or leaking pipes, a full home PEX repipe can greatly improve your plumbing system.     Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Lake Oswego, OR

    Gray polybutylene pipe was common in homes built from the late 1970s through the mid 1990s and is notorious for sudden failures. Chlorine in municipal water breaks down the plastic and its fittings over time. Many insurers now ask about polybutylene when writing a policy. Replacing it with PEX removes that risk and can make your home easier to insure and sell.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Lake Oswego, OR

    PEX-A, such as Uponor, uses an expansion tool to stretch the pipe and a ring over the fitting, which then shrinks back into a tight seal. The fittings have a larger inner diameter, so flow restriction is lower. PEX-A is also more flexible and can recover from kinks with a heat gun. It is our favored choice for many full repipes.
