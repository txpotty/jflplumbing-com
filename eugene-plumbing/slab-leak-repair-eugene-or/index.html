    Pipe Rerouting and Repiping in Eugene

    For more extensive damage, multiple leaks, or pipes that are too old and corroded to repair reliably, pipe rerouting is a long-term solution. This involves abandoning the leaking line under the slab and installing a new pipe through the walls or attic. This method bypasses the problem area entirely, providing a fresh, durable plumbing line. It is a common solution for aging homes in Eugene with original copper or galvanized plumbing.

    Spot Repair Through the Slab in Eugene, OR

    When the leak is isolated and the rest of the pipe is in good shape, a spot repair through the slab is a practical option. We cut a small opening, dig down to the pipe and replace the damaged section. The trench is compacted and the concrete is patched. Flooring is then restored by your preferred contractor.

    Slab Leak Repair for PEX and Copper Lines in Eugene, OR

    Whether your home in Eugene, OR has modern PEX piping or traditional copper lines, our plumbers are proficient in repairing both. Pinhole leaks are common in older copper due to corrosive soil conditions and water chemistry, while PEX can sometimes suffer from installation issues or rodent damage. We carry a full range of parts and tools for both materials, making for a correct and durable repair no matter your pipe type.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Repair in Eugene

    Cold water slab leaks are harder to feel but still waste water and wet the soil. They often show up as a high water bill or a meter that moves with all fixtures off. We isolate the cold line, locate the leak and repair or reroute it. Fixing cold leaks promptly helps protect your foundation.

    Direct Slab Penetration Repair in Eugene, OR

    When a slab leak is directly accessible and localized, direct slab penetration is often the most efficient repair method. This involves carefully cutting a small section of your concrete slab to access and repair or replace the damaged pipe section. We take great care to contain dust and debris during this process in your Eugene home.
