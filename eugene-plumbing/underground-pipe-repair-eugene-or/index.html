    Trenchless Pipe Repair in Eugene

    Trenchless pipe repair offers a less invasive alternative to traditional excavation for buried pipe issues. Methods like pipe bursting or pipe lining allow us to repair or replace damaged pipes with minimal digging, preserving your landscaping, driveways and foundations in Eugene. This approach saves time and reduces restoration costs, making it a great fit for many homeowners.

    Underground Sewer Line Repair in Eugene, OR

    Buried sewer laterals crack from root intrusion, soil settling and age. We camera the line to find the exact spot, then excavate and replace the damaged section with PVC. Shielded couplings make a watertight connection to the existing pipe. We confirm proper slope and flow before backfilling and compacting the trench.

    Pipe Bursting Services in Eugene, OR

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless technique where a new pipe is pulled through the path of the old, broken pipe, fracturing the old pipe outward at the same time. This method is highly effective for complete pipe replacement, creating a new, durable pipe of the same or larger diameter. It's a solid solution for severely damaged or collapsed sewer and water lines, offering a long-term fix in Eugene, OR.

    Pool and Outbuilding Supply Line Repair in Eugene, OR

    Many properties have buried water lines feeding a pool auto-fill, a guest house, an outdoor kitchen or a detached garage. These lines are usually longer than the main service and harder to locate. We use tracer wire and acoustic equipment to find leaks along the full run. Repairs are made with direct-bury rated pipe and tested before we close up.

    Irrigation and Yard Line Repair in Eugene

    Drip lines and sprinkler supply pipes are often shallow and easy to break during landscaping or by roots. A broken irrigation main can run up a water bill fast in summer. We repair PVC and poly irrigation mains, replace failed backflow connections and fix the supply line to the valve manifold. We can also reroute vulnerable lines away from trees.

    Galvanized and Polybutylene Line Replacement in Eugene

    Galvanized steel pipe rusts from the inside, and polybutylene pipe becomes brittle and splits without warning. If your underground service is either material, repairs are often temporary. We replace the line with PEX or Type K copper, often by pulling the new pipe through the old path to limit digging. A new line restores pressure and removes the risk of repeated leaks.
