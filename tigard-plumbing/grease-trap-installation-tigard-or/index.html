    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Interceptor Installation in Tigard

    Hydro-mechanical grease interceptors, often called HGI units, are compact and designed for indoor use, typically under a sink or on the floor. These units use baffles to slow down wastewater flow, allowing grease to separate and accumulate. JFL Plumbing installs various sizes of HGI units, ensuring proper venting, leveling, and connection to your drainage system in Tigard for the best efficiency.

    Gravity Grease Interceptor Installation in Tigard, OR

    Gravity grease interceptors, or GGI units, are larger, usually located outdoors, and rely on the natural difference in specific gravity between FOG and water. These are common for high-volume commercial kitchens. Our team handles the involved excavation, concrete work, and plumbing connections required for proper GGI installation, ensuring compliance with all local Oregon ordinances for waste management.
