    Sump Pump Installation Cost Comparison in Gresham, OR

    Replacing a pump in an existing pit is the least expensive option, typically a few hundred dollars plus the pump.     Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Gresham, OR

    Submersible sump pumps are designed to sit directly in the sump pit, submerged in water, which makes them quieter and less intrusive. They are powerful and effective for basements that take on a lot of water. Our team in Gresham, OR will install your submersible pump with precision, making sure of proper sealing, discharge piping and electrical connections for quiet, reliable operation, which matters most in finished basements.

    Pedestal Sump Pumps in Gresham

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor above the pit, which makes them easier to service and a solid fit for narrow pits in older homes. They typically last a long time because the motor never sits in water. The tradeoff is more noise and a less finished look, so we usually recommend them for utility rooms rather than living spaces. We install them with a rigid discharge, a quality check valve and a float rod adjusted to your pit depth.
