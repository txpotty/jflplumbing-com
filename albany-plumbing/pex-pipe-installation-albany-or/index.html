    Copper to PEX Conversions in Albany

    Copper lines can develop pinhole leaks from water chemistry, high velocity and sometimes soil contact under slabs. Soft water can be slightly corrosive, which may lead to pinhole leaks in copper pipe and blue-green stains on fixtures. Once a home has had several pinhole leaks, more usually follow. Converting to PEX stops the cycle and reduces the risk of hidden water damage. We use approved transition fittings wherever new PEX connects to existing copper stubs or valves.

    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Albany, OR

    JFL Plumbing is knowledgeable in installing all types of PEX piping, including PEX-A, PEX-B and PEX-C, each with distinct properties for specific applications. We help you choose the best PEX type for your home's needs and budget in Albany, OR. Our experience ensures the correct PEX material and fitting method, whether it is expansion for PEX-A or crimp and clamp for PEX-B and PEX-C, for a secure and lasting system.

    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Albany

    We offer installation for both PEX-A and PEX-B piping and understand the different advantages of each. PEX-A, often preferred for its flexibility and ability to recover from minor kinks, uses expansion fittings. PEX-B is more rigid but also very durable, typically using crimp or compression fittings. Our plumbers in Albany can help you decide which type of PEX suits your project, considering factors like budget and application.
