    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Eugene

    We offer installation for both PEX-A and PEX-B piping and understand the different advantages of each. PEX-A, often preferred for its flexibility and ability to recover from minor kinks, uses expansion fittings. PEX-B is more rigid but also very durable, typically using crimp or compression fittings. Our experts in Eugene can help you decide which type of PEX suits your project, considering factors like budget and application.

    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Eugene, OR

    JFL Plumbing is experienced with installing all types of PEX piping, including PEX-A, PEX-B and PEX-C, each with distinct properties for specific applications. We help you choose the best PEX type for your home's needs and budget in Eugene, OR. Our experience ensures the correct PEX material and fitting method, whether it is expansion for PEX-A or crimp and clamp for PEX-B and PEX-C, for a secure and lasting system.

    Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Eugene, OR

    Gray polybutylene pipe was common in homes built from the late 1970s through the mid 1990s and is known for sudden failures. Chlorine in municipal water breaks down the plastic and its fittings over time. Plenty of insurers now ask about polybutylene when writing a policy. Replacing it with PEX removes that risk and can make your home easier to sell and insure.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Eugene, OR

    PEX-A, such as Uponor, uses an expansion tool to stretch the pipe and a ring over the fitting, which then shrinks back into a secure seal. The fittings have a larger inner diameter, so flow restriction is lower. PEX-A is also more flexible and can recover from kinks with a heat gun. It is our favored choice for many full repipes.
