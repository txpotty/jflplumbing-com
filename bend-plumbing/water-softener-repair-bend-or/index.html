    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Bend, OR

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates suction to draw brine into the resin tank. Sediment and salt residue clog its tiny ports. When it clogs, the brine tank fills with water instead of emptying. We clean or replace the injector and screen and check the brine line for air leaks.

    Control Valve Repair in Bend

    The control valve is the brain of your water softener, directing the flow of water and starting regeneration cycles. When it malfunctions, your system can stop softening water or waste salt. JFL Plumbing technicians are experts at diagnosing and repairing common control valve issues, whether mechanical or electronic, ensuring precise timing and efficient operation. We stock common replacement parts to speed up your repair in Bend.

    Control Valve Troubleshooting & Repair in Bend, OR

    The control valve is the brain of your water softener, regulating the flow of water and starting regeneration cycles.
