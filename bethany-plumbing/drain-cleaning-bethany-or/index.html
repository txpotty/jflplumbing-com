    Hydro jetting is usually safe for most modern plumbing systems. However, it may not suit severely corroded or fragile pipes, which are sometimes found in very old Bethany properties. We always perform a camera inspection first to check pipe condition and use the safest method.

    For most homes, an annual or twice-yearly professional drain cleaning is enough, especially for kitchen and main lines. If you have frequent clogs or an older plumbing system, more frequent cleanings can be helpful to prevent major issues.

    Tree roots are drawn to the moisture and nutrients inside sewer lines. During dry periods, roots will seek out any tiny crack or loose joint in older pipes to reach water, then grow and expand, causing significant blockages. This is a common issue for homes with mature trees.

    We generally advise against chemical drain cleaners. While they might offer a temporary fix, they can corrode pipes, especially older ones, and are often useless against tough clogs like tree roots or heavy grease buildup. They can also pose health risks.

    Hydro jetting is recommended for tough, persistent clogs, especially those caused by heavy grease buildup, mineral deposits, or significant tree root intrusion. It provides a more complete cleaning than traditional snaking, scouring the entire pipe interior.

    A plunger or a small hand auger can handle a simple hair clog near the drain. For deep clogs, main line backups or repeat problems, a pro has the machines and camera to find and fix the real cause.
