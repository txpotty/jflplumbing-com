    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Hillsboro

    Many plumbing codes require the installation of an expansion tank with a new water heater, particularly in closed-loop systems. Oregon enforces a statewide plumbing specialty code based on the Uniform Plumbing Code, and plumbers must hold a state license. An expansion tank protects your water heater and plumbing system from excessive pressure buildup, which can be caused by the heating process. Our installation service includes correctly sizing and installing the expansion tank to support proper system operation and prevent early wear on your new water heater and other plumbing fixtures in Hillsboro. This small component offers significant long-term protection.

    Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in Hillsboro, OR

    Electric tank water heaters are common in homes without natural gas service. We install models with the correct voltage and breaker size for your electrical panel. These units do not need venting, which allows more flexible placement. We also install a drain pan when the heater is in a closet or attic. Electric models are simple, reliable and quiet.

    Expansion Tank Installation in Hillsboro, OR

    Many homes have a pressure reducing valve or check valve that creates a closed system. When water heats and expands, pressure can rise and stress pipes and relief valves. An expansion tank absorbs that extra pressure. We install and pre-charge the tank to match your water pressure. This protects your new water heater and plumbing.

    Attic and Closet Water Heater Installs in Hillsboro

    Some local homes have water heaters in attics or interior closets. These installs require careful planning for access, drain pans and relief line routing. Attic temperatures can swing widely with the seasons, so we take extra steps to protect connections. We install drain pans with proper drain lines to protect ceilings. If relocation makes sense, we can move the heater to a better spot.

    New water heaters typically come with a manufacturer's warranty on the tank and components, usually ranging from 6 to 12 years.     We install Rheem, A.O. Smith, Bradford White, Navien, Rinnai and other trusted brands. We recommend models based on reliability, warranty and budget.

JFL Plumbing handles all necessary permit applications and inspections in Hillsboro as part of our service.

    Most households of two to four people do well with a 40 or 50 gallon tank. Larger families may need a 75 gallon tank or a tankless unit sized by flow rate.
