    Some older homes still have Orangeburg pipe, a tar-paper material that flattens and blisters over time.     Sewer Pipe Relining in Redmond, OR

    Sewer pipe relining is a trenchless repair method where a new, durable pipe is created within the existing damaged one. A flexible, epoxy-impregnated liner is inserted into the old pipe and inflated, curing in place to form a seamless, strong and corrosion-resistant new pipe. This method is effective for addressing cracks, minor breaks and root intrusion without extensive digging. It extends the life of your sewer system and is an excellent option for preserving landscapes in Redmond.

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water and solids. Bellies often form where the original bedding washed out or where the soil settled after heavy rain. Clay soils are common in the western valleys of Oregon, and they stay wet for months, stressing sewer lines and foundations.
