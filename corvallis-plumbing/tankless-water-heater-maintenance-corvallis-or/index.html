    Frequently Asked Questions About Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Corvallis

    Once a year is standard, and homes with very hard water and no softener may need it every six to nine months. Heavy users and commercial units need it more frequently.

    Most tankless water heater manufacturers recommend annual maintenance to ensure good performance and extend the unit's lifespan. In areas with harder water, more frequent checks, perhaps every 6 to 9 months, might be worth it to prevent excessive scale buildup.

    Most visits take one to two hours, including the descaling cycle. Installing isolation valves on the same trip adds about an hour.

    A cold water sandwich can come from restricted flow, a dirty inlet filter or a flow sensor not reading correctly. Descaling and filter cleaning usually improve it.

    While some basic tasks like cleaning external filters might be possible for a homeowner, complete maintenance, especially descaling the heat exchanger and inspecting gas lines, should be left to a licensed professional.
