    Frequently Asked Questions About Backflow Preventer Installation in Gresham

    Residential pressure vacuum breakers typically cost $250 to $700 installed. Double check and RPZ assemblies for larger homes or businesses range from $800 to $3,500 or more depending on size and enclosure needs.

    Backflow is when water flows the wrong way through a pipe and carries contaminants into the clean water supply. It can happen from back siphonage during a pressure drop or back pressure from pumps and boilers.

    It depends on the hazard and local rules. Irrigation usually uses a PVB or double check, while high hazard connections like commercial kitchens or chemical injection require an RPZ.

    We install backflow preventers from leading manufacturers known for quality and reliability. Our go-to brands include Watts, Febco, Zurn and Apollo, so you receive a durable, effective device that meets all industry standards.

    Most water providers require testable assemblies to be tested at installation and then every year. Some high hazard facilities may be tested more often.

    An RPZ, or reduced pressure zone device, provides the highest level of protection and is used for high-hazard applications where contaminants could be toxic. A DCVA, or double check valve assembly, is used for low-hazard applications where contaminants are non-toxic. The choice comes down to the risk of contamination at the cross-connection point.

    Many jurisdictions require a permit for new backflow assemblies, especially on commercial properties. We check local rules and pull the permit when needed.

    Backflow preventers are installed at cross-connection points where non-potable water could enter the potable water supply.
