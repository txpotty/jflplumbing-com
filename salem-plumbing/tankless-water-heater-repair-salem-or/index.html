    Reliable Tankless Water Heater Repair Services in Salem, OR

    Flow Sensor and Thermistor Repair in Salem

    Tankless heaters use flow sensors to start and thermistors to regulate temperature. A dirty inlet filter or failed sensor causes no hot water, fluctuating temperature or the unit turning off mid-shower. We clean filters, test sensor readings and replace failed parts. Temperature becomes steady again once readings are correct.

    Tankless Descaling and Flushing in Salem

    Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium scale inside the heat exchanger, limiting flow and causing overheating. Many units warn of this with scale codes, such as the LC code on Rinnai models. We connect a circulation pump to the service valves and run a descaling solution through the exchanger. Annual descaling is wise for most homes on hard water.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Repair in Salem

    Gas tankless water heaters require precise gas pressure and proper ventilation for safe operation. Issues can range from gas valve failures to carbon monoxide sensor errors. Our certified technicians in Salem perform thorough diagnostics on gas lines, igniters and exhaust systems, so your unit is both functional and safe.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Repair in Salem, OR

    Rinnai is a popular tankless brand, and its units can face challenges such as scaling from hard water or issues with their flame rod. Our technicians in Salem, OR are skilled at Rinnai tankless water heater repair, focusing on descaling services, fan motor replacements and resolving issues with gas valve assemblies. We make sure your Rinnai system continues to provide reliable, on-demand hot water, following manufacturer specifications.

    Recirculation Pump Repair in Salem

    Many tankless systems use internal or external recirculation to deliver hot water faster. Pump failures, check valve problems or wrong schedule settings can cause long waits or lukewarm water. We test the pump, check valves and controls and set the schedule to match your routine. A properly tuned system saves both water and gas.

    Condensing Tankless Repair in Salem, OR

    Condensing tankless heaters, such as many Navien models, extract extra heat from exhaust and produce condensate. Clogged condensate traps, failed neutralizers and blocked drains can shut the unit down. We clean the trap, replace the neutralizer media and confirm proper drainage. PVC venting is also checked for slope and sagging.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Repair in Salem, OR

    Electric tankless units offer a compact solution but can encounter problems with heating elements or circuit breakers. JFL Plumbing offers expert electric tankless water heater repair in Salem, OR, addressing electrical faults, ensuring proper wiring and replacing faulty elements to restore your hot water supply safely and efficiently.

    Tankless Water Heater Error Code Diagnostics in Salem, OR

    Modern tankless water heaters display error codes to flag specific problems, from flow rate issues to combustion errors. Our experts in Salem, OR are trained to interpret these codes accurately, using dedicated diagnostic tools to pinpoint the exact component failure. This accuracy allows us to quickly order the correct parts and perform targeted repairs, saving you time and money. We address codes related to burner failures, fan issues and temperature sensor errors, among others.
