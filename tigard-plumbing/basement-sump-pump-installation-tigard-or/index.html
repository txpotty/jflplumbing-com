    Sump Pump Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Tigard, OR

    New Sump Pit Excavation in Tigard

    If your basement has never had a pump, we start by finding the low point where water collects. We saw cut the slab, excavate a pit roughly 18 to 24 inches wide and 24 to 30 inches deep, and set a perforated or solid liner depending on whether we are collecting groundwater or surface seepage. Gravel goes around the liner to help water flow in while keeping fine soil out. We then patch the slab flush with new concrete so the floor stays clean and level.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Basement Sump Pump Installation in Tigard

    The cost for basement sump pump installation in Tigard, OR usually ranges from $800 to $2,500, depending on the type of pump, pedestal or submersible, whether a new pit needs to be excavated and whether a battery backup is included. We provide itemized estimates after an on-site assessment.

    Manufacturer warranties usually range from 3 to 5 years on quality cast iron pumps. We also back our installation labor, and we will walk you through the specific coverage for the model you choose.

    Usually, standard home insurance policies do not cover a new sump pump installation or repairs unless the damage was caused by a sudden, accidental event like a burst pipe, rather than gradual wear and tear or groundwater seepage. Some policies offer separate water backup or sump pump failure endorsements for an additional premium. It is best to check with your insurance provider.

    Discharge should go to your yard, a dry well or an approved drainage area at least 10 feet from the foundation. Connecting a sump pump to the sanitary sewer is usually not allowed by local codes.

    We routinely install Zoeller, Liberty Pumps and Wayne because they offer cast iron housings, reliable switches and good parts support. We will fit the brand and model to your pit size and budget.

    While it might seem like a DIY project, basement sump pump installation involves complex plumbing, electrical work and proper discharge routing. Incorrect installation can lead to pump failure, electrical hazards or poor water removal, which can cause serious flood damage. It is always best to hire a licensed plumber for a correct, code-compliant installation in Tigard.
