    Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Oregon City

    Pipe bursting replaces the old sewer line by pulling a new pipe through the path of the old one. A bursting head breaks the old pipe outward while fused HDPE pipe follows close behind. This typically needs only an entry pit and an exit pit instead of a long trench. It works well under driveways, mature trees and landscaping. The new pipe is seamless, which leaves no joints for roots to enter.

    Sewer Pipe Lining as an Alternative in Oregon City

    Cured-in-place pipe lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. After cleaning, a resin-soaked liner is installed and cured in place. This works when the host pipe still holds its shape without major sags or collapse. Lining can be a smart option under slabs or finished areas. We tell you plainly if your line is a candidate.

    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Oregon City

    Cast iron drains in older homes corrode from the inside, particularly along the bottom of the pipe. Over time this leaves cracks, holes and a rough surface that snags waste.
