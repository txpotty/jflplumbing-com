    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Portland

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE series or Rinnai Sensei reach efficiencies above 90 percent by capturing heat from exhaust gases. They vent through inexpensive PVC and produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized and drained properly. We install a neutralizer cartridge and route the condensate line to an approved drain. Damp crawlspaces are common, and constant moisture can corrode exposed metal pipe and fittings over time. Condensing units still save gas because they work year-round on showers, laundry and dishes.     Frequently Asked Questions About Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Portland

    The cost for tankless water heater replacement in Portland, OR typically ranges from $3,000 to $6,000, depending on the unit's brand, capacity, fuel type (gas or electric) and any needed changes to gas lines, electrical or venting. This estimate includes both the new unit and professional installation. We will provide a detailed quote upfront after assessing your needs.

    Most manufacturers offer 10 to 15 years on the heat exchanger and about 5 years on parts for residential use. We add our own labor warranty and register the unit for you.

    A standard tankless water heater replacement, if you are upgrading from an existing tankless unit, usually takes 4 to 8 hours. If you are converting from a traditional tank water heater, the process can take longer, possibly a full day, due to extra plumbing, gas or electrical changes required for proper installation and venting.

    To protect the life and efficiency of your new tankless water heater, regular maintenance is key, especially in areas with hard water. This typically involves annual flushing to remove mineral buildup, checking the air filter, and inspecting gas and water connections. JFL Plumbing offers maintenance plans to help keep your unit in top condition.

    Well-maintained tankless units can last 15 to 20 years. In hard water areas without regular descaling, heat exchangers can fail far sooner. Annual flushing makes the biggest difference.

    JFL Plumbing installs and replaces all major trusted brands of tankless water heaters. Our preferred brands include Rinnai, Navien, Noritz and Rheem, as they are known for their reliability, energy efficiency and long-term performance. We can also order and install other brands based on your preference or home requirements.

    With proper installation and regular maintenance, a new tankless water heater typically lasts 20 to 25 years. This is much longer than traditional tank water heaters, which usually last 10 to 15 years. Regular flushing and descaling can help extend the life of your tankless unit, especially in hard water areas.

    Most jurisdictions in Oregon require a permit for water heater replacement, especially when gas piping or venting changes. We handle permits where they are required.
