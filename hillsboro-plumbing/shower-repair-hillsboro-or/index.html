    Shower Valve Replacement in Hillsboro

    If you are experiencing inconsistent water temperature, low water pressure, or difficulty turning your shower on or off, the shower valve may be failing. A faulty mixing valve can lead to scalding or freezing water, which is a safety hazard. We can replace old or broken shower valves with new, reliable models, including pressure-balancing and thermostatic options for improved safety and comfort. This often involves accessing the valve behind the shower wall.

    Hot Water Pressure Issues in Hillsboro, OR

    Lack of hot water or weak hot water pressure in your shower can be incredibly frustrating. This can stem from several issues, including a failing water heater, sediment buildup in the pipes, or a problem with the shower mixing valve itself. Our technicians will diagnose whether the issue is with your water heater, a blocked pipe, or a faulty component within the shower. We provide lasting solutions to restore consistent hot water flow and pressure.

    Shower Faucet Leak Fixes in Hillsboro

    Beyond the shower head, leaks can occur at the faucet handles or behind the wall. These leaks can be harder to spot but can cause serious damage if left unaddressed. We use advanced diagnostic tools to pinpoint the exact location of the leak and provide precise repairs, often involving O-ring replacement or tightening connections, preventing expensive water damage to your home's structure.

    Tub and Shower Combination Repair in Hillsboro, OR

    Many homes in Hillsboro, OR feature tub and shower combination units, which means their plumbing is connected. Issues like faulty diverter valves, shared drain clogs, or a leaky tub spout can affect the entire unit. Our plumbers are skilled at diagnosing and repairing problems specific to these combination fixtures, ensuring both your tub and shower work properly. We handle all components, from the faucet to the overflow drain.

    Shower Pan and Wall Leak Repair in Hillsboro, OR

    Leaks under the shower pan or behind tile can cause rot, mold and stains on ceilings below. We use moisture meters and flood tests to find failed grout, cracked pans or leaking pipe joints. Then we repair the source, whether that means resealing a drain, replacing a valve or rebuilding a pan. On slab homes, these leaks can damage flooring in nearby rooms.

    Showerhead and Arm Repair in Hillsboro

    Dripping showerheads, leaking arm connections and broken handheld hoses are frequent problems. We clean or replace showerheads, reseal shower arms and install new handheld hoses and holders. We can also add a slide bar for a handheld shower. Hard water clogs nozzles, so we recommend heads with flexible rubber nozzles that are easy to clean.

    Shower Diverter Repair in Hillsboro, OR

    A diverter sends water from the tub spout to the showerhead. When it wears out, water keeps running from the spout and shower pressure drops. We replace diverter spouts, three-handle diverter stems and multi-function diverter valves. This restores full flow to the showerhead.

    Leaky Shower Faucet Repair in Hillsboro

    A dripping shower faucet can waste hundreds of gallons of water a year and drive up your utility bill. Our plumbers quickly identify the source of the leak, which is often a worn-out O-ring, washer, or cartridge within the faucet assembly. We carry a wide range of replacement parts for common brands like Delta, Moen, Kohler, and Price Pfister, allowing for same-day repair. We will make sure the new components are correctly installed, stopping the drip and saving you water.
