    Frequently Asked Questions About Toilet Installation in Eugene

    Labor to install a customer-supplied standard toilet typically runs $200 to $400. Including a mid-range toilet, most installs total $450 to $900, while wall-hung systems with carriers range from $1,500 to $3,500 or more. We give you a flat price up front.

    JFL Plumbing stands behind our workmanship. We offer a warranty on all our toilet installations, covering labor and making sure your new toilet works as expected. Specific warranty details will be explained at the time of service.

    Toilets carry manufacturer warranties, often 1 to 5 years on parts and longer on porcelain. We also guarantee our installation workmanship and will come back to fix any leak caused by the install.

    A one-piece toilet has the bowl and tank integrated into a single unit, offering a sleek look and easier cleaning. A two-piece toilet has a separate bowl and tank, which are bolted together during installation. Both types are widely available and perform well.

    Common signs you might need a new toilet include frequent clogs, cracks in the porcelain bowl or tank, constant running water, a wobbly base, or a toilet that is very old and inefficient. Upgrading can save water and refresh the look of your bathroom.

    The rough-in is the distance from the finished wall to the center of the drain, usually 12 inches but sometimes 10 or 14. Buying a toilet with the wrong rough-in means it will not fit or will sit too far from the wall.

    Replacing a toilet in the same location generally does not need a permit, but new bathrooms, additions and drain relocations typically do. Requirements vary by city, and we check with the local building department for your project.

    JFL Plumbing installs all major toilet brands, including Kohler, Toto, American Standard, Delta, and Gerber. We can help you choose a model that fits your budget, style preferences, and water efficiency goals.
