    Emergency Slab Leak Repair in Portland, OR

    A large slab leak can push water up through flooring or into walls quickly. We help you shut off the water and send a technician to isolate the leaking line. Often we can restore water to the rest of the house while we plan the repair. Prompt action limits flooring damage and soil saturation.

    Slab Leak Pipe Rerouting in Portland

    For more complex or recurring slab leaks, or when the original piping is badly deteriorated, pipe rerouting is an excellent long-term solution. This method involves abandoning the leaky pipe under the slab and installing new plumbing lines through walls or the attic, bypassing the foundation entirely. This removes future slab leak risks from that particular line and is often preferred for older homes in Portland with aging copper or galvanized pipes.

    Slab Leak Detection in Portland

    We start by shutting off fixtures and watching the meter to confirm a leak. Then we isolate the hot and cold lines to determine which one is leaking. Using acoustic ground microphones, we listen for the sound of escaping water through the concrete. Thermal imaging shows hot water leaks as warm areas on the floor surface.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Repair in Portland

    Cold water slab leaks are harder to feel but still waste water and wet the soil. They often show up as a high water bill or a meter that moves with all fixtures off. We isolate the cold line, locate the leak and repair or reroute it. Fixing cold leaks quickly helps protect your foundation.

    Slab Leak Reroute in Portland

    Rerouting is often the best long-term choice, especially with older copper lines. We abandon the leaking line under the slab and run a new PEX line through the attic and walls. Openings are small and placed where patching is easiest. Reroutes end future leaks in that section of pipe.

    Sewer Line Slab Leak Repair in Portland, OR

    Slab leaks aren't always about clean water pipes, since sewer lines can also leak under your foundation. These leaks pose significant health risks and can cause extensive damage. We specialize in detecting and repairing sewer line slab leaks, using camera inspections to identify the exact problem area. Our methods restore proper waste removal and prevent contamination in your Portland, OR property, following health and safety codes.

    Pipe Rerouting and Repiping in Portland

    For more extensive damage, multiple leaks, or pipes that are too old and corroded to repair reliably, pipe rerouting is a long-term solution. This involves abandoning the leaking line under the slab and installing a new pipe through the walls or attic. This method skips the problem area entirely, providing a fresh, durable plumbing line. It is a common solution for aging homes in Portland with original copper or galvanized plumbing.

    Direct Slab Penetration Repair in Portland, OR

    When a slab leak is directly accessible and localized, direct slab penetration is often the most practical repair method. This involves carefully cutting a small section of your concrete slab to access and repair or replace the damaged pipe section. Our team makes sure the repair is fully compliant with Oregon plumbing codes and the slab is correctly restored, preventing future issues. We take great care to contain dust and debris during this process in your Portland home.
