    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Bethany

    PEX-B is stiffer and more affordable, and it is joined with copper crimp rings or stainless steel cinch clamps over brass or poly fittings. It is a solid option for smaller projects and budget-conscious repipes. Brands like Viega, Zurn and SharkBite offer reliable PEX-B systems. We use a go/no-go gauge on every crimp to verify a proper seal.

    PEX for Water Line Replacement in Bethany

    When your main water line from the street to your home needs replacement, PEX is an excellent option. Its flexibility allows for easier trenchless or minimal-dig installations, reducing disruption to your yard in Bethany. PEX main lines handle ground movement and resist corrosion better than rigid pipe. Hillside homes can face soil movement after heavy rain, which can shift buried water and sewer lines.

    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Bethany, OR

    JFL Plumbing is knowledgeable in installing all types of PEX piping, including PEX-A, PEX-B and PEX-C, each with distinct properties for specific applications. We help you choose the best PEX type for your home's needs and budget in Bethany, OR.     Hydronic Heating with PEX in Bethany

    PEX is also a leading choice for hydronic heating systems, including radiant floor heating.     Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Bethany, OR

    Gray polybutylene pipe was common in homes built from the late 1970s through the mid 1990s and is known for sudden failures. Chlorine in municipal water breaks down the plastic and its fittings over time. Many insurers now ask about polybutylene when writing a policy. Replacing it with PEX removes that risk and can make your home easier to sell and insure.

    Copper to PEX Conversions in Bethany

    Copper lines can develop pinhole leaks from water chemistry, high velocity and sometimes soil contact under slabs. Soft water can be slightly corrosive, which may lead to pinhole leaks in copper pipe and blue-green stains on fixtures. Once a home has had several pinhole leaks, more usually follow. Converting to PEX stops the cycle and lowers the risk of hidden water damage. We use approved transition fittings wherever new PEX connects to existing copper stubs or valves.

    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in Bethany, OR

    A manifold system sends a dedicated line from a central hub to each fixture, much like a breaker panel for water. Each line gets its own shutoff, which makes repairs easier and keeps pressure steady when several fixtures run. Home-run layouts use more pipe but fewer fittings in walls. We often combine manifold and trunk-and-branch designs to balance cost and performance.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Bethany, OR

    PEX-A, such as Uponor, uses an expansion tool to stretch the pipe and a ring over the fitting, which then shrinks back into a secure seal. The fittings have a larger inner diameter, so flow restriction is lower. PEX-A is also more flexible and can recover from kinks with a heat gun. It is our preferred choice for many full repipes.
