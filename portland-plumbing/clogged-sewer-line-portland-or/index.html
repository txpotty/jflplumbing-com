    Tree Root Invasion Removal in Portland

    Main Line vs. Branch Line Clogs in Portland, OR

    Understanding the difference between a main sewer line clog and a branch line clog is key for proper diagnosis and repair in Portland, OR. A main line clog affects all drains in your home, often causing backups in lower-level fixtures. Branch line clogs are usually isolated to a single fixture or area. Our technicians are skilled at finding the exact location of the blockage, whether it is in the main line or a specific branch, for a targeted and efficient repair.

    Main Line Hydro Jetting in Portland

    Hydro jetting uses water at thousands of psi through a specialized nozzle to cut roots, grease and scale. It cleans the full diameter of the pipe rather than just boring a hole through the clog. We check the pipe condition with a camera first so jetting is only used on lines that can handle it. Many homeowners find that jetting buys them years before another backup.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Portland

    Before any work begins, a sewer camera inspection is key for accurately diagnosing the cause and location of a clog. Our high-resolution cameras provide a clear view inside your sewer lines, allowing us to identify issues like cracks, breaks, root intrusions, or excessive grease. This non-invasive method saves time and money by avoiding unnecessary digging, ensuring a precise repair plan for your Portland property. It is essential for older homes with cast iron or clay pipes.

    Tree Root Sewer Clogs in Portland

    Roots from thirsty trees and shrubs seek out moisture and push into sewer pipe joints. Clay and older cast iron pipe are especially prone to root intrusion because of their many joints.     Grease and Wipes Sewer Blockages in Portland, OR

    Cooking grease cools and hardens in the main line, and so-called flushable wipes do not break down the way toilet paper does.
