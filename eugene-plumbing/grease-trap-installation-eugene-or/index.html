    Frequently Asked Questions About Grease Trap Installation in Eugene

    The cost of grease trap installation in Eugene, OR varies widely based on the size, type, and complexity of the system. Small under-sink units might range from $1,000 to $3,000, while large outdoor interceptors requiring excavation can cost anywhere from $5,000 to $20,000 or more. We provide transparent, upfront pricing after a thorough on-site assessment.

    Installation time depends on the size and complexity of the grease trap. A small under-sink unit might take a few hours, while a large outdoor interceptor could require several days due to excavation and concrete work. We aim to complete installations efficiently to minimize disruption to your business operations.

    We install all common types of grease traps, including gravity grease interceptors, hydro-mechanical grease traps (automatic grease removal units), and compact under-sink grease traps. Our team helps you determine the best system for your commercial kitchen's specific needs and local code requirements.

    An indoor under-sink trap can often be installed in a day. Outdoor interceptors usually take several days to a week or more, depending on excavation, concrete work and inspection scheduling.

    While the terms are often used interchangeably, 'grease trap' typically refers to smaller, indoor hydro-mechanical units, often found under sinks. 'Grease interceptor' usually refers to larger, outdoor gravity-fed units with higher capacities. Both serve the same purpose of separating FOG from wastewater.

    Manufacturer warranties vary, with many polyethylene units carrying long structural warranties. We also guarantee our installation workmanship and connections.

    Signs you might need a new grease trap include frequent sewer backups, persistent foul odors coming from drains, slow drainage throughout your kitchen, or if your current unit is visibly corroded or leaking. If your existing trap is old and no longer meets current local codes in Eugene, an upgrade is also advisable.

    JFL Plumbing provides a warranty on our workmanship for all grease trap installations. Additionally, the grease trap unit itself will usually come with a manufacturer's warranty. We will provide you with all warranty details at the time of installation, ensuring peace of mind.
