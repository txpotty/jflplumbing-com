    Frequently Asked Questions About Hydro Jetting in Tigard

    The cost of hydro jetting in Tigard, OR typically ranges from $300 to $800, depending on the severity of the clog, the length and diameter of the pipe, and ease of access.     While small, low-pressure drain jetters are available for rent, professional hydro jetting equipment is far more powerful and requires training to operate safely and effectively. Improper use can damage pipes, cause injury, or fail to clear the blockage. For severe clogs, it is always best to rely on experienced professionals like JFL Plumbing.

    Most residential jobs take 1 to 3 hours including inspection. Long commercial lines or heavy grease can take longer.

    Hydro jetting is highly effective against a wide range of stubborn clogs that traditional snaking cannot fully clear. This includes heavy grease buildup, mineral scale, sludge, soap scum, hair clogs, and even invasive tree roots that have penetrated your sewer lines. It is designed to scour the entire pipe surface.

    Snaking uses a rotating cable to break through a clog. Jetting uses pressurized water to clean the entire pipe wall and wash debris away.

    No permit is needed for jetting. If the inspection shows damage that needs repair, permits may be required and we handle them.
