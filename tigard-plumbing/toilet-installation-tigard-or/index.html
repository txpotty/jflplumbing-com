    Frequently Asked Questions About Toilet Installation in Tigard

    Labor to install a customer-supplied standard toilet typically runs $200 to $400. Including a mid-range toilet, most installs total $450 to $900, while wall-hung systems with carriers range from $1,500 to $3,500 or more. We give you a firm price up front.

    While it's possible for a homeowner to attempt a DIY toilet installation, it's usually best left to professionals. Improper installation can lead to leaks, wobbling, inefficient flushing, and potential water damage. Professional plumbers ensure proper sealing, secure mounting, and adherence to codes.

    The rough-in is the distance from the finished wall to the center of the drain, typically 12 inches but sometimes 10 or 14. Buying a toilet with the wrong rough-in means it will not fit or will sit too far from the wall.

    A one-piece toilet has the bowl and tank integrated into a single unit, offering a sleek look and easier cleaning. A two-piece toilet has a separate bowl and tank, which are bolted together during installation. Both types are widely available and perform well.

    Soft water can be slightly corrosive, which may lead to pinhole leaks in copper pipe and blue-green stains on fixtures. Hard water can lead to mineral buildup in toilet tanks and bowls over time, affecting performance and requiring more regular cleaning. While it doesn't directly impact installation, choosing a toilet with a good glaze and strong flush can help reduce these effects.

    While DIY toilet installation is possible, hiring a professional makes sure the job is done correctly. Professionals have the tools and expertise to prevent leaks, get proper sealing, and comply with local plumbing codes, saving you potential headaches and expensive repairs down the road.

    A comfort height toilet has a bowl that is taller than standard models, usually around 17 to 19 inches from the floor to the top of the seat. This height is closer to a standard chair, making it more comfortable for many adults, especially those with mobility issues.

    Toilets carry manufacturer warranties, often 1 to 5 years on parts and longer on porcelain. We also guarantee our installation workmanship and will return to fix any leak caused by the install.
