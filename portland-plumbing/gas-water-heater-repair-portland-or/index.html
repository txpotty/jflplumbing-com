    Dependable Gas Water Heater Repair Services in Portland, OR

    Flammable Vapor Sensor Repair in Portland

    Most gas water heaters built in recent decades have a flammable vapor ignition resistant design. A sensor or flame arrestor screen shuts the burner down if it detects vapors or overheating. Lint and dust in garages can clog the arrestor and shut down the system. We clean the combustion chamber and replace sensors or thermal switches as required.

    Pilot Light & Burner Issues in Portland

    A common problem with gas water heaters is a pilot light that won't stay lit or a burner that fails to ignite. This can be caused by a faulty thermocouple, a dirty pilot tube or issues with the gas control valve. Our technicians in Portland are adept at diagnosing these components and performing the necessary repairs, whether it is cleaning, adjusting or replacing parts. We confirm proper gas flow and ignition for consistent hot water.

    Cost-Effective Gas Water Heater Solutions in Portland, OR

    Tank Leaks & Pressure Problems in Portland

    Even gas water heaters can develop tank leaks, often due to corrosion from hard water or high water pressure. Many rural homes rely on private wells, and owners around Portland benefit from testing for iron, hardness and other typical concerns. While some leaks may call for a replacement, minor leaks or pressure relief valve issues can often be repaired. Our team checks the extent of the damage, inspects the anode rod and addresses pressure imbalances to prevent further problems in your Portland home.

    Power Vent Water Heater Repair in Portland

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust out through PVC pipe. Failures usually involve the blower motor, pressure switch, hot surface igniter or condensate in the vent. We read the error codes, test each part and repair the fault. Correct vent slope and termination are checked before we leave.

    Gas Water Heater Noise Troubleshooting in Portland

    Strange noises coming from your gas water heater, such as rumbling, popping or whistling, often indicate sediment buildup in the tank or a burner problem. Hard water can add to sediment accumulation. Our repair service includes flushing the tank and inspecting for sediment, addressing pressure issues or checking other components that might be causing the noise, restoring efficiency and quiet operation.

    Gas Valve and Thermostat Repair in Portland, OR

    Issues with the gas valve or thermostat can lead to inconsistent water temperatures or a complete lack of hot water. Our team can test these key components for proper operation, checking for electrical continuity and gas flow. If a part is defective, we will replace it with a quality, manufacturer-approved component so your water heater heats evenly and safely.
