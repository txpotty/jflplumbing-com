    PEX Repiping for Slab Leaks in Albany, OR

    If multiple slab leaks point to a systemic issue with your home's original plumbing, a full PEX repipe might be the most cost-effective and durable solution. PEX tubing is flexible, corrosion-resistant, and less prone to leaks caused by foundation shifts, making it a good choice for many homes. We can replace all the water supply lines in your Albany home, providing a brand-new, leak-free plumbing system that will last for decades.

    Pipe Rerouting and Repiping in Albany

    For more extensive damage, multiple leaks, or pipes that are too old and corroded to repair reliably, pipe rerouting is a long-term solution. This involves abandoning the leaking line under the slab and installing a new pipe through the walls or attic. This method skips the problem area entirely, providing a fresh, durable plumbing line. It is a common solution for aging homes in Albany with original copper or galvanized plumbing.

    Direct Slab Penetration Repair in Albany, OR

    When a slab leak is directly accessible and localized, direct slab penetration is often the most efficient repair method.     Tunneling Slab Leak Repair in Albany, OR

    In some cases, especially when the leak is under a load-bearing wall or in a difficult-to-access area, tunneling beneath the foundation is the preferred method. This lets our plumbers reach the damaged pipe from outside your home, avoiding disruption to your interior floors and finishes. Tunneling spares the interior but calls for skilled excavation and shoring to protect structural integrity throughout the repair process in Albany, OR.
