    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Hillsboro, OR

    Many homes have tankless heaters mounted on an exterior wall with no venting at all, since the unit exhausts directly outside.     Condensing Tankless Replacement in Hillsboro

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE series or Rinnai Sensei reach efficiencies above 90 percent by capturing heat from exhaust gases. They vent through low-cost PVC and produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized and drained properly. We install a neutralizer cartridge and route the condensate line to an approved drain. The eastern side of the state is drier, with hotter summers and colder winters that stress pipes and water heaters differently. Condensing units still save gas because they work year-round on showers, laundry and dishes. We explain the cost difference so you can decide if the efficiency gain is worth the price.
