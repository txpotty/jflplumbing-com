    Frequently Asked Questions About Sewer Line Replacement in Bend

    Sewer line replacement in Bend typically ranges from about $4,000 to $15,000 for a typical residential lateral, and can run higher for long, deep or hard-to-reach lines. Trenchless work is often priced around $80 to $250 per foot depending on conditions.

    With trenchless methods, usually only two small pits are dug. Open-trench work disturbs more, but we backfill and restore the area when finished.

    Traditional replacement involves digging a trench to access and replace the entire pipe, which can be more disruptive to your yard. Trenchless methods, like pipe bursting or pipe lining, require minimal digging, often just two access points, protecting your landscaping while installing a new pipe.

    We provide a written workmanship warranty, and the pipe materials carry their own manufacturer warranty. We review the terms with you before work begins.

    The duration depends on the complexity and method. A trenchless replacement might take 1 to 3 days, while a traditional trenched replacement could take 3 to 5 days, sometimes longer for larger projects. We will give you a clear timeline estimate during your consultation.

    We usually use PVC for open-trench work and HDPE for pipe bursting. Both resist corrosion and root intrusion much better than clay or cast iron.

    With traditional trenching, some disruption to your yard is unavoidable, but we work to limit it and restore the area as well as possible. If trenchless methods are suitable, yard damage is greatly reduced, often limited to small access points.

    We primarily use high-quality PVC (polyvinyl chloride) or HDPE (high-density polyethylene) pipe for sewer line replacements. These materials are durable, corrosion-resistant, flexible, and long-lived, making them ideal for the soil conditions in Oregon.
