    Tankless Water Heater Error Code Diagnostics in Portland, OR

    Modern tankless water heaters display error codes to indicate specific problems, from flow rate issues to combustion errors. Our experts in Portland, OR are trained to interpret these codes accurately, using specialized diagnostic tools to pinpoint the exact component failure. This precision allows us to quickly order the correct parts and perform targeted repairs, saving you time and money. We address codes related to burner failures, fan issues and temperature sensor errors, among others.

    Ignition Failure Repair in Portland, OR

    If your tankless unit will not fire, the cause may be a dirty flame rod, a bad igniter, low gas pressure or a blocked intake. We test each component and measure gas pressure at the inlet. Undersized gas lines are a common hidden cause when a tankless unit replaced a tank. We correct the issue so the unit lights reliably.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Repair in Portland, OR

    Rinnai is a popular tankless brand, and its units can face challenges such as scaling from hard water or issues with their flame rod. Our technicians in Portland, OR are adept at Rinnai tankless water heater repair, focusing on descaling services, fan motor replacements and resolving issues with gas valve assemblies. We ensure your Rinnai system continues to provide reliable, on-demand hot water, following manufacturer specifications.

    Flow Sensor and Thermistor Repair in Portland

    Tankless heaters use flow sensors to start and thermistors to regulate temperature. A dirty inlet filter or failed sensor causes no hot water, fluctuating temperature or the unit turning off mid-shower. We clean filters, test sensor readings and replace failed parts. Temperature becomes steady again once readings are correct.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Repair in Portland

    Gas tankless water heaters require correct gas pressure and proper ventilation for safe operation. Issues can range from gas valve malfunctions to carbon monoxide sensor errors. Our certified technicians in Portland perform thorough diagnostics on gas lines, igniters and exhaust systems, so your unit is both functional and safe.

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling Service in Portland

    Hard water can lead to mineral buildup, or scaling, in your tankless water heater's heat exchanger, reducing efficiency and eventually causing breakdowns. Soft water can be slightly corrosive, which may lead to pinhole leaks in copper pipe and greenish stains on fixtures. Our descaling service in Portland involves flushing the unit with a specialized cleaning solution to dissolve these mineral deposits. This preventative maintenance and repair step is key for extending the lifespan of your tankless heater and keeping it at peak performance.

    Tankless Descaling and Flushing in Portland

    Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium scale inside the heat exchanger, restricting flow and causing overheating. Many units warn of this with scale codes, such as the LC code on Rinnai models. We connect a circulation pump to the service valves and run a descaling solution through the exchanger. Annual descaling is a smart move for most homes on hard water.

    Condensing Tankless Repair in Portland, OR

    Condensing tankless heaters, such as many Navien models, extract extra heat from exhaust and produce condensate. Clogged condensate traps, failed neutralizers and blocked drains can shut the unit down. We clean the trap, replace the neutralizer media and confirm proper drainage. PVC venting is also inspected for slope and sagging.
