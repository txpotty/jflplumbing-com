    Water Line Replacement vs. Repair in Eugene, OR

    Deciding between pipe repair and full replacement depends on several factors, including the age of your pipes, the extent of the damage, and the frequency of past issues. A small, isolated leak might be repaired, but widespread corrosion or multiple leaks usually warrant replacement. JFL Plumbing provides straightforward assessments in Eugene, OR, explaining the pros and cons of each option.     PEX Pipe Installation in Eugene

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping is a popular choice for modern pipe replacement projects in Eugene due to its flexibility, durability, and resistance to corrosion and freezing. It's also less expensive to install than copper. Many rural homes rely on private wells, and owners around Eugene benefit from testing for iron, hardness and other common concerns. JFL Plumbing expertly installs PEX piping for full home repipes or targeted section replacements, providing a reliable and long-lasting plumbing solution that handles local water conditions well.

    PEX Repiping in Eugene

    PEX-A is flexible, resists scale buildup and expands slightly if water freezes, which helps in colder homes. We use expansion fittings that keep the full inside diameter of the pipe for better flow. Systems can be run as a home-run manifold with a shutoff for each fixture or as a trunk and branch layout. PEX installs faster than copper with far fewer fittings in the walls. It is our most popular repipe material.

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Eugene

    Gray polybutylene pipe was installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid-1990s. It breaks down from the inside when exposed to chlorinated water, and fittings can crack with no warning. Some insurers raise premiums or decline coverage on homes that still have it. We remove polybutylene supply lines and replace them with PEX-A or copper. A completed repipe can also help when listing the home.

    Burst Pipe Repair & Replacement in Eugene, OR

    Western valleys see only occasional hard freezes, but ice storms and cold snaps still burst pipes in crawlspaces and exterior walls. Cold snaps can lead to burst pipes, especially in exposed areas or unheated spaces. When a pipe bursts in your Eugene, OR home, it causes immediate water damage and requires urgent attention. Our team provides emergency service to shut off water, remove damaged sections, and replace them with new, more freeze-resistant piping, often PEX, to prevent future occurrences. We work fast to minimize disruption and water loss.

    Copper Pinhole Leak Replacement in Eugene, OR

    Pinhole leaks in copper usually start as a small green stain or a damp spot on drywall. They are caused by a mix of water chemistry, high velocity and thin Type M pipe, and once they begin they tend to keep coming back. Patching each leak costs $300 to $800 per visit plus drywall repair. We can replace the affected branch or repipe the home with PEX, which does not develop pinholes. We help you weigh the cost of both paths.
