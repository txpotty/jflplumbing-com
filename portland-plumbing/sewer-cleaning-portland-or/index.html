    Hydro Jetting for Sewer Lines in Portland

    For the most stubborn sewer line clogs, hydro jetting is the gold standard. This powerful technique uses high-pressure water streams to blast away grease, scale, mineral buildup, and even tree roots from the interior walls of your sewer pipes. JFL Plumbing uses professional hydro jetting equipment for sewer cleaning in Portland, delivering a thorough cleaning that leaves your pipes nearly spotless, greatly reducing the chance of future blockages and extending the life of your sewer system.

    Preventative Sewer Maintenance in Portland

    Tree Root Removal in Portland, OR Sewers

    Tree roots are a common cause of sewer line blockages, especially in Portland, OR neighborhoods where mature trees are common. Roots seek out moisture and can work into even tiny cracks in sewer pipes, growing and eventually blocking the entire line. JFL Plumbing uses dedicated root cutting tools attached to our drain augers and high-pressure hydro jetting to remove these invasive roots, restoring flow and preventing further damage. We can also explain long-term solutions to protect your pipes.

    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Lines in Portland

    Older properties may still have vitrified clay or even Orangeburg fiber pipe running to the street. Clay joints are open invitations for roots, and Orangeburg can flatten under soil weight. We clean these lines gently and use the camera to map joints and soft spots. If the pipe is failing, we will tell you plainly and show the footage. Cleaning keeps you running while you plan a replacement.
