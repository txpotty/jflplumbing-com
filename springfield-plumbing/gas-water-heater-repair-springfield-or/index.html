    Reliable Gas Water Heater Repair Services in Springfield, OR

    Venting and Draft Repair in Springfield, OR

    Atmospheric gas water heaters rely on a properly sloped metal flue to carry exhaust out of the home. Disconnected sections, rusted draft hoods and poor slope can let combustion gases spill back. We repair or replace vent sections and confirm draft with a test. Proper venting is essential for your family's safety.

    Gas Control Valve Replacement in Springfield, OR

    The gas control valve controls temperature and shuts off gas if the pilot goes out. Newer valves from Honeywell and White-Rodgers use status lights that blink codes when something fails. If the valve itself has failed, we replace it with an exact match or approved universal model. We leak test and confirm temperature control afterward.

    Gas Water Heater Code Compliance in Springfield, OR

    Any repair involving gas appliances must meet strict local and state plumbing codes. JFL Plumbing ensures all gas water heater repairs in Springfield, OR are performed in full compliance with current regulations. This includes proper venting, gas line connections and seismic strapping where required. Our attention to code ensures not only safety but also a smooth path through any future inspections.

    Gas Valve and Thermostat Repair in Springfield, OR

    Issues with the gas valve or thermostat can lead to uneven water temperatures or a complete lack of hot water. Our team can test these crucial components for proper operation, checking for electrical continuity and gas flow. If a part is defective, we replace it with a quality, manufacturer-approved component so your water heater heats evenly and safely.

    Gas Water Heater Repair Cost in Springfield, OR

    Thermocouple and pilot repairs typically cost $150 to $350. Gas control valve replacement often runs $350 to $700, and power vent blower repairs can range from $400 to $800. A new gas tank installed usually costs $1,600 to $3,000 depending on size and venting. We help you choose based on tank age and condition.

    Pilot Light Repair in Springfield

    A pilot that will not light or will not stay lit is the most frequent gas water heater call. Causes include a worn thermocouple, a clogged pilot orifice, a bad piezo igniter or a tripped thermal cutoff. We test the thermocouple output with a meter and service or replace the pilot assembly. Most pilot repairs are finished in about an hour.

    Propane Water Heater Repair in Springfield

    Rural properties often run water heaters on propane instead of natural gas. Propane units use different orifices and regulator pressures, and improper conversions cause sooting and poor performance. We confirm the correct parts are installed and repair pilots, valves and burners. We also check tank regulators for correct pressure.

    Power Vent Water Heater Repair in Springfield

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust out through PVC pipe. Failures often involve the blower motor, pressure switch, hot surface igniter or condensate in the vent. We read the error codes, test each part and repair the failure. Correct vent slope and termination are checked before we leave.
