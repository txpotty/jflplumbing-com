    Exterior Foundation Leak Solutions in Portland

    Many foundation leaks start from exterior factors like poor drainage, overflowing gutters, or saturated soil. We assess your home's exterior to identify these culprits. Our solutions include grading adjustments, French drain installation, and downspout extensions to redirect water away from your foundation. Good exterior water management is crucial for preventing hydrostatic pressure buildup against your foundation walls in Portland and keeping water out.

    Slab Leak Detection & Repair in Portland

    Slab leaks are notorious for being difficult to detect, often showing subtle signs like warm spots on the floor or a sudden jump in your water bill. JFL Plumbing uses acoustic and infrared technology to precisely locate these hidden leaks under your concrete foundation. Once identified, we offer tailored repair solutions, whether it's a spot repair, re-routing, or pipe bursting, to restore your plumbing system in Portland without extensive damage to your property.

    Slab Leak Detection and Repair in Portland

    Slab leaks are common in homes built on concrete foundations, often caused by shifting soil or corroded pipes underneath. Our technicians use low-impact methods like electronic leak detection and hydrostatic pressure testing to find these hidden leaks. Once located, we can perform targeted repairs, either by slab tunneling or direct access, limiting disruption to your home. We work with many pipe materials, including copper, PEX, and PVC, ensuring a durable repair for your Portland property.

    Direct Foundation Leak Repair in Portland, OR

    When the leak is in an accessible spot and the rest of the pipe is in good condition, direct repair is often the most economical choice. We cut a small opening in the floor, excavate to the pipe and replace the damaged section. The soil is compacted and the concrete is patched to match. Flooring can then be restored by your flooring contractor.

    You can check your water meter with all fixtures off to confirm a leak. Locating the exact spot under a foundation requires specialized equipment and training.

    If you see significant cracks, doors sticking or uneven floors, a structural engineer can assess the foundation. We stop the plumbing leak first so the soil can stabilize.

    Depending on the leak's location, some foundation leak repairs may involve digging or breaking concrete, particularly for slab leaks or exterior drainage issues.     Simple repairs often do not require a permit, but reroutes and repipes may, depending on your city. We pull permits when required.
