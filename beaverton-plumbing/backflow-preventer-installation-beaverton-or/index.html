    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Beaverton

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of backflow protection and are commonly required for high-hazard applications in Beaverton. These devices feature two independent check valves and a relief valve that opens to discharge water if pressure differentials indicate a backflow condition. JFL Plumbing specializes in precise RPZ installation, with proper sizing, placement and testing to meet all regulatory requirements for residential and commercial properties. We work with trusted brands like Watts and Febco.

    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Beaverton

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines. They have two spring-loaded check valves and test cocks for annual certification. Unlike an RPZ, they do not discharge water, so they can be installed in vaults or below grade where local rules permit. We size them to avoid excessive pressure loss on your system.
