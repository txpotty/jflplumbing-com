    Deciding between repair and full replacement depends on the extent of the damage.     Galvanized steel pipe was common in homes built before the 1970s. Over time, it corrodes inside and narrows the pipe. This causes low pressure and discolored water. We replace galvanized lines with modern PEX or copper. The difference in water pressure is often immediate.
