    PEX and HDPE Water Line Installation in Eugene, OR

    PEX and HDPE are flexible, which means fewer fittings in the ground and better tolerance for soil movement. HDPE is especially durable for long runs and resists cracking in expansive clay. PEX must stay out of sunlight before burial, so we store and lay it carefully on every job site.
