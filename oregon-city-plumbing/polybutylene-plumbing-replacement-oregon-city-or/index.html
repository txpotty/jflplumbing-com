    Understanding Polybutylene Failure in Oregon City

    Polybutylene's built-in weakness stems from its chemical reaction with oxidants like chlorine, commonly used in municipal water treatment. Over time, this reaction causes the pipes and their plastic fittings to become brittle, leading to hairline cracks and eventual rupture. Many rural homes rely on private wells, and owners around Oregon City benefit from testing for iron, hardness and other typical concerns. Hard water can also add stress to pipe that is already breaking down.
