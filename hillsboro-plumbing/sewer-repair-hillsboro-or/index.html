    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Hillsboro

    Clay and cast iron sewer pipe crack from soil movement, heavy vehicles and simple age. A cracked section lets sewage leak into the soil and lets dirt fall into the pipe, which leads to recurring clogs. We excavate at the marked spot, cut out the damaged length and install new Schedule 40 PVC with shielded couplings rated for the transition. The repair is tested before we backfill so you know it holds.

    Sewer Line Leak Detection and Repair in Hillsboro, OR

    Undetected sewer line leaks can lead to unpleasant odors, wet spots in your yard and even structural damage to your home's foundation. JFL Plumbing uses precise leak detection methods to pinpoint the source of the leak, whether it's a minor crack or a major break. Once found, we provide targeted repairs, from patching small leaks to replacing compromised sections of pipe, ensuring a watertight system.

    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Hillsboro

    Hydro-jetting is a powerful cleaning method that uses high-pressure water streams to clear stubborn blockages, grease buildup and even tree roots from sewer lines. It's much more effective than snaking for thorough cleaning and stopping recurring clogs. This service is especially helpful for restaurants and commercial properties in Hillsboro dealing with heavy grease accumulation, as well as homes with recurring drain issues.

    Pipe Burst and Collapse Repair in Hillsboro, OR

    A burst or collapsed sewer pipe can lead to significant damage and unsanitary conditions. JFL Plumbing offers emergency repair services to promptly address these critical failures.     Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Hillsboro

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg pipe, a tar-paper material that flattens and blisters over time. Cast iron from the same era corrodes from the inside and scales up until little flow remains. When a camera shows widespread failure, patching is a waste of money. We replace the line in sections or end to end with PVC, and we can usually use trenchless methods to avoid tearing up the whole yard.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Hillsboro, OR

    Tree and shrub roots go right for the moisture and nutrients leaking from old sewer joints. Once inside, they grow into dense mats that catch paper and waste. We cut the roots with a mechanical cutter or hydro-jetter, then repair or seal the joint so they cannot come right back. For lines with many rooted joints, we will show you when lining or replacement makes more sense than ongoing cleaning.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Hillsboro, OR

    For many sewer line issues, especially those caused by tree roots or pipe corrosion, trenchless repair offers a less invasive solution. This method, which includes pipe bursting or pipe lining, requires minimal excavation, saving your yard and landscaping from extensive disruption. We use specialized equipment to either pull a new pipe through the old one or insert a resin-coated liner that cures in place, creating a new, durable pipe inside the old one. It's an effective and popular choice for preserving property appearance in Hillsboro.
