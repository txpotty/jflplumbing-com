    Resin Bed Cleaning and Regeneration Optimization in Oregon City, OR

    The resin beads are responsible for ion exchange, removing hard minerals from your water. Over time, these beads can become fouled with iron or other contaminants, reducing their effectiveness. Our maintenance includes cleaning the resin bed and adjusting the regeneration cycle settings for full softening capacity. We make sure your system is regenerating efficiently for your water quality, providing truly soft water.

    Resin Bed Cleaning and Replacement in Oregon City

    Softener resin can foul with iron, manganese or sediment and lose capacity over time. Chlorine and chloramine in city water also slowly break resin down. We run a resin cleaner through the system and test performance afterward. If the resin is exhausted, we rebed the tank with fresh high-capacity resin, which costs much less than a new system.

    Brine Tank Cleaning and Inspection in Oregon City, OR

    Over time, sludge and insoluble materials can accumulate in your brine tank, hindering the softening process. Our maintenance includes thoroughly cleaning the brine tank, inspecting for any cracks or leaks and ensuring the float valve operates correctly. This step is essential for the efficient regeneration of your water softener in Oregon City, OR.
