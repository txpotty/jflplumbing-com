    Complete Tankless Water Heater Maintenance Services in Oregon City, OR

    Noritz Tankless Water Heater Tune-Ups in Oregon City, OR

    Noritz tankless water heaters are robust systems, but they still require annual tune-ups to maintain their performance through every season. Our maintenance includes checking the unit's exhaust system, ensuring proper combustion air intake and verifying the integrity of the heat exchanger. We also calibrate the temperature and flow settings for maximum efficiency and comfort, preventing common issues like cold water sandwiches.

    Recirculation and Combi System Service in Oregon City, OR

    Many tankless units now include built-in recirculation pumps or buffer tanks to deliver faster hot water. These systems move more water through the exchanger and scale up sooner. We flush the recirculation loop, check pump operation and verify crossover or dedicated return line settings. Correct schedules can reduce wasted energy while still giving you quick hot water.

    Inlet Filter and Air Intake Cleaning in Oregon City

    Every tankless unit has an inlet water screen that catches grit and scale flakes. Those screens can clog with sand and debris, especially after municipal line work. We pull and clean the screen and inspect the air intake filter, which can load up with dust and dryer lint. Clean filters keep the flow sensor reading accurately and the burner breathing properly.

    Heat Exchanger Descaling in Oregon City

    The heat exchanger in a tankless heater is a coil of small tubes that water races through while the burner fires. Hard water drops calcium and magnesium on those hot surfaces, narrowing the passages over time. We connect a submersible pump and hoses to the service valves and circulate descaling solution for 45 to 60 minutes. Then we flush with fresh water to rinse the system thoroughly. Restored flow means more stable temperatures and fewer shutdowns.

    Condensing Unit and Neutralizer Service in Oregon City

    High-efficiency condensing tankless heaters produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized before it goes down a drain. We inspect the condensate trap, line and neutralizer cartridge and replace the media when it is spent. A clogged trap can cause shutdowns or water leaking inside the cabinet. We also check that the line is shielded where it runs outdoors.

    Navien Tankless Heater Tune-Ups in Oregon City

    Navien tankless water heaters often feature condensing technology, making them highly efficient but also requiring particular maintenance. Our tune-ups include checking the condensate trap for blockages, cleaning the secondary heat exchanger and verifying the operation of the circulator pump. We also ensure a proper air-fuel mixture and calibrate sensors for peak efficiency in Oregon City homes.

    Isolation Valve Kit Installation in Oregon City, OR

    Some tankless heaters were installed without service valves, which makes proper flushing very difficult. We install a set of isolation valves with purge ports on the hot and cold lines so future maintenance takes minutes. The kit also lets you shut off water to the heater without closing the main. It is a one-time upgrade that pays for itself in simpler service.

    Annual Tankless Water Heater Inspection in Oregon City

    An annual inspection of your tankless water heater in Oregon City is a proactive measure against costly repairs. Our thorough check includes examining the gas line connections for leaks, testing the pressure relief valve, inspecting the igniter and cleaning the air intake filter. We look for any signs of wear or potential failure, providing recommendations to keep your system running smoothly all year long, through hot spells and cold snaps alike.
