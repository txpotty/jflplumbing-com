    Trenchless Sewer Repair Options in Gresham, OR

    Where applicable, we offer trenchless sewer repair methods like pipe bursting. This technique involves pulling a new pipe through the old one, at the same time breaking the existing pipe apart. Trenchless methods are ideal for certain types of pipe damage and property layouts in Gresham.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Gresham, OR

    Some lines have severe sagging, wrong slope or are too shallow for trenchless methods. In these cases, open-trench replacement is the best option.
