    Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Eugene, OR

    Thermal imaging cameras allow us to visualize temperature differences on surfaces, which can indicate a hidden water leak. As water escapes a hot water line, it creates a clear thermal signature on the concrete slab. This technique is particularly useful for hot water line leaks and helps us narrow down the search area significantly without any physical disruption to your property in Eugene.

    Video Pipe Inspection for Sewer Leaks in Eugene, OR

    Sewer line issues under a slab can also mimic slab leak symptoms. We use high-resolution video cameras to inspect the interior of your drain lines, identifying cracks, blockages or separations. This is important if the issue isn't a pressurized water line, helping us determine if a sewer line repair is needed instead for your Eugene home.

    Emergency Slab Leak Detection in Eugene

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Eugene

    Rerouting abandons the leaking line under the slab and runs new pipe through the walls or attic. It avoids breaking concrete and often costs less than direct access under finished floors. We typically use PEX for reroutes because it handles heat and resists hard-water corrosion. This option is a good fit when the slab is covered in tile or hardwood.
