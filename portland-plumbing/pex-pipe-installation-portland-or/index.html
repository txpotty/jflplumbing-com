    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in Portland, OR

    A manifold system sends a dedicated line from a central hub to each fixture, similar to a breaker panel for water. Each line gets its own shutoff, which makes repairs easier and keeps pressure steady when several fixtures run. Home-run layouts use more pipe but fewer fittings in walls. We often combine manifold and trunk-and-branch designs to balance cost and performance.

    Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Portland, OR

    Gray polybutylene pipe was common in homes built from the late 1970s through the mid 1990s and is notorious for sudden failures. Chlorine in municipal water breaks down the plastic and its fittings over time. Many insurers now ask about polybutylene when writing a policy. Replacing it with PEX removes that risk and can make your home easier to insure and sell.

    Remodel and Addition PEX Rough-In in Portland

    Kitchen and bathroom remodels are a good time to replace old supply lines with PEX. We rough in new lines to the correct heights for vanities, showers, tubs and appliances, then pressure test before the walls are closed. Additions and guest suites get properly sized lines tied into your existing system. We coordinate with your contractor to keep the project schedule moving.

    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Portland

    We offer installation for both PEX-A and PEX-B piping and understand the different advantages of each. PEX-A, often preferred for its flexibility and ability to recover from minor kinks, uses expansion fittings. PEX-B is more rigid but also very durable, typically using crimp or compression fittings. Our experts in Portland can help you decide which type of PEX suits your project, considering factors like budget and application.

    PEX for Remodels & Additions in Portland, OR

PEX is an ideal choice for these projects in Portland, OR due to its adaptability and ease of installation in existing wall cavities or new structures. We can cleanly tie new PEX lines into your current system, ensuring adequate water supply to new fixtures and appliances without compromising your existing plumbing.
