    Copper Pinhole Leak Replacement in Corvallis, OR

    Pinhole leaks in copper usually start as a small green stain or a damp spot on drywall. They are caused by a mix of water chemistry, high velocity and thin Type M pipe, and once they begin they tend to keep appearing. Patching each leak costs $300 to $800 per visit plus drywall repair. We can replace the affected branch or repipe the home with PEX, which does not develop pinholes. We help you weigh the cost of both paths.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Corvallis

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping has become a popular choice for residential pipe replacement due to its flexibility, affordability, and resistance to freezing. Western valleys see only occasional hard freezes, but ice storms and cold snaps still burst pipes in crawlspaces and exterior walls. Unlike rigid pipes, PEX can expand, reducing the risk of burst pipes during unexpected cold snaps. JFL Plumbing specializes in PEX installation, which often allows for less invasive replacement methods compared to traditional materials.     Gray polybutylene pipe was installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid-1990s.
