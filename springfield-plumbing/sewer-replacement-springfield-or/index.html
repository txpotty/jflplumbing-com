    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Springfield

    For significant damage or older, failing systems, traditional sewer line replacement often involves excavating a trench to access and replace the entire pipe. This method is effective for replacing severely collapsed or extensively rooted lines, especially common in older Springfield homes with cast iron or clay pipes. We carefully plan the excavation to minimize disruption to your yard and landscaping, using heavy-duty equipment for efficient digging and backfilling.

    Code-Compliant Sewer Replacement in Springfield

    All sewer replacement work performed by JFL Plumbing in Springfield closely adheres to local plumbing codes and regulations. This includes proper pipe sizing, depth, slope, and connection methods. Obtaining the necessary permits and ensuring inspections are passed is a critical part of our process. We handle all the paperwork and coordination, ensuring your new sewer line is not only functional but also fully compliant with all municipal requirements, avoiding future issues or penalties.

    Addressing Tree Root Intrusions in Springfield, OR

    Tree roots are a common cause of sewer line damage, especially with older, more porous pipe materials like clay or cast iron. Roots can penetrate tiny cracks, grow inside the pipe, and eventually cause blockages or even burst the line. Our sewer replacement services specifically address root-damaged pipes, often recommending more resistant materials like PVC or ABS to prevent future intrusions. We also advise on tree placement to protect your new sewer system.

    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Springfield

    Cast iron sewer pipe can corrode from the inside, creating rough surfaces and holes. Scale buildup catches debris and causes clogs. We replace cast iron with PVC or HDPE. This restores flow and ends corrosion. Many homes built before 1975 have cast iron lines.

    Clay and Orangeburg Pipe Replacement in Springfield, OR

    Clay pipe joints let roots in, while Orangeburg pipe made from tar paper deforms over time. Both are likely to fail. We replace these lines with modern materials. Camera inspection shows the full extent of damage. Replacement ends recurring root problems.

    Sewer Cleanout Installation in Springfield, OR

    A cleanout provides simple access for future cleaning and inspection. Many older homes lack an exterior cleanout. We install one during sewer replacement. This saves time and money on future maintenance. It also helps in emergencies.

    Sewer Line Material Upgrades in Springfield

    Many older homes in Springfield still have original clay or cast iron sewer lines, which are prone to root intrusion, corrosion, and cracking. JFL Plumbing can upgrade your system to modern PVC (polyvinyl chloride) piping. PVC is durable, resistant to root penetration, and has a smooth interior that lowers the likelihood of blockages, providing a long-lasting solution for your home's drainage.

    Commercial Sewer Replacement in Springfield

    Commercial buildings often have larger sewer lines and grease issues. We replace commercial laterals and work with businesses to reduce downtime. We may install grease interceptors as needed. We handle permits and city coordination. Our crews work efficiently to restore service.
