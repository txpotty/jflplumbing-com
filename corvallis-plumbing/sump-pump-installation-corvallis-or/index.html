    Replacing a pump in an existing pit usually costs $600 to $1,500. Installing a new pit with slab cutting and excavation often costs $1,500 to $4,000. Battery backups typically add $500 to $1,500.

    Most standard sump pump installations can be completed within 3 to 6 hours.     Most manufacturers offer warranties from 3 to 5 years, and some premium models carry longer coverage. We also warranty our installation workmanship.

    We install Zoeller, Wayne, Liberty and other dependable brands. We recommend models based on your pit size and water inflow.

JFL Plumbing handles all necessary permits and inspections for your project in Corvallis.

    A well-maintained sump pump usually lasts about 7 to 10 years. However, its lifespan can vary based on usage frequency, water quality and pump quality. Regular inspections by a professional can help determine if your sump pump in Corvallis is nearing the end of its service life.

    A submersible sump pump sits entirely inside the sump pit, submerged in water, making it quieter and taking up less space.
