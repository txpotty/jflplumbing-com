    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Services in Albany

    A typical drain cleaning for a single clogged fixture can take anywhere from 30 minutes to 2 hours, depending on the severity and location of the clog. More complex issues, like main sewer line clearing or hydro jetting, may require several hours. We provide an expected timeline after our initial assessment.

    Most single fixture drain cleanings take under an hour. Main line cleaning and hydro jetting usually take one to three hours.

    Hair, grease, soap scum and hard water scale cause most fixture clogs. Main line clogs are often caused by wipes, tree roots or a sag in the pipe.

    Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to clean the interior surfaces of your pipes, thoroughly removing grease, scale, and even tree roots. It is safe for most modern plumbing systems. Our technicians will perform a camera inspection first to ensure your pipes are structurally sound enough to withstand the process.
