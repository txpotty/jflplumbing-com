    Sewer Pipe Relining in Eugene, OR

    Sewer pipe relining is a trenchless repair method where a new, durable pipe is created within the existing damaged one. A flexible, epoxy-impregnated liner is inserted into the old pipe and inflated, curing in place to form a seamless, strong and corrosion-resistant new pipe. This method is effective for addressing cracks, minor breaks and root intrusion without extensive digging. It extends the life of your sewer system and is an great option for preserving landscapes in Eugene.

    Sewer Line Leak Detection & Repair in Eugene, OR

    Undetected sewer line leaks can lead to unpleasant odors, wet spots in your yard and even structural damage to your home's foundation. JFL Plumbing uses accurate leak detection methods to pinpoint the source of the leak, whether it's a minor crack or a major break.     Sewer Repair Cost Comparison in Eugene, OR

    A single spot repair is the most affordable fix when the rest of the line is sound. We lay out all three options with real numbers so you can weigh cost against how long the repair will last.

    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Eugene, OR

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water and solids. Bellies often form where the original bedding washed out or where the soil settled after heavy rain. Hillside homes can face soil movement after heavy rain, which can shift buried water and sewer lines. We confirm the depth of standing water on camera, then re-bed and re-grade that section to restore proper fall. Correct slope is typically a quarter inch per foot for a typical residential line.

    Sewer Vent Pipe Repair in Eugene, OR

    While often overlooked, the sewer vent pipe is crucial for proper drainage and preventing sewer gas buildup. Issues with vent pipes, such as blockages from animal nests or damage from storms, can lead to slow drains, gurgling noises and foul odors. JFL Plumbing diagnoses and repairs vent pipe problems, ensuring your entire drainage system works correctly and safely and maintaining good indoor air quality.
