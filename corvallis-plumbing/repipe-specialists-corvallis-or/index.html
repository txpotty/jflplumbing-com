    Repiping is a complex plumbing project that requires specialized tools, knowledge of plumbing codes, and significant experience. It's highly recommended to hire professional repipe specialists like JFL Plumbing to make sure the job is done correctly, safely, and to code.

    We provide a written workmanship warranty, and PEX manufacturers such as Uponor offer long-term warranties when certified installers use their system. We explain the details before we start.

    Common signs you need repiping include persistent low water pressure throughout your home, discolored or rusty-looking water, frequent pipe leaks, visible corrosion on exposed pipes, and a metallic taste or odor in your water. If your home is over 50 years old with original pipes, it's a smart move to have them inspected, especially if they are galvanized or polybutylene.

    Soft water can be slightly corrosive, which may lead to pinhole leaks in copper pipe and blue-green stains on fixtures. Hard water can lead to mineral buildup, or scale, inside pipes over time. This buildup constricts water flow, reduces pressure, and can accelerate corrosion, especially in older metal pipes. Repiping with materials like PEX or properly installed copper, along with considering a water softener, can mitigate these effects and extend the life of your plumbing system.

    Both PEX and copper are strong choices for repiping in Oregon, each with distinct advantages.
