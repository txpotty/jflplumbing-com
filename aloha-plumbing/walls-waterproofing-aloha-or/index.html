    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Aloha, OR

    Block and stem walls often show efflorescence, which is the white mineral crust left when water passes through masonry. Irrigation and sprinklers aimed at the house are common culprits. We adjust or reroute irrigation, clean the wall and apply a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating. Proper grading and drip line placement help keep the wall dry for good.

    Sump Pump Installation and Repair in Aloha, OR

    Sump pumps are a key part of many interior waterproofing systems, particularly in homes that see heavy rainfall. We install high-capacity sump pumps designed to handle large volumes of water, keeping your basement dry even during heavy storms. Our services include primary sump pump installation, battery backup sump pump systems for power outages, and routine maintenance or repair of existing units. We make sure the discharge line is routed correctly so water does not circulate back toward the foundation.

    Laundry Room and Utility Wall Waterproofing in Aloha, OR

    Washing machine hoses, utility sinks and water heaters can all leak into nearby walls. We replace burst-prone rubber hoses with braided stainless hoses and install leak pans and shutoff valves. Walls around water heaters and laundry boxes can be sealed to resist water damage. These small upgrades head off costly drywall repairs.

    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Aloha, OR

    A cracked or poorly sloped shower pan often causes wet baseboards and stained ceilings below. We test the pan by plugging the drain and flooding it to verify the leak. Then we rebuild the pan with correct pre-slope, a new liner or bonded membrane and a properly clamped drain. A second flood test confirms the pan is watertight before tile.

    Plumbing Leak Behind Walls in Aloha

    A slow drip from a shower valve, supply stub or drain fitting can soak a wall for months before you notice. We find the leak with moisture meters, thermal imaging and pressure tests. The damaged section of pipe or valve is replaced, and wet drywall is cut out and dried. Once the cavity is dry, we restore the wall and seal any wet-area walls.

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Aloha

    Many homes in Aloha have crawl spaces, which are very prone to moisture issues. Installing a heavy-duty vapor barrier on crawl space walls and floors is a key waterproofing strategy. This thick polyethylene sheeting prevents ground moisture from evaporating into the crawl space, protecting subflooring and joists and improving indoor air quality. It's an effective way to control humidity and prevent wood rot.

    Waterproofing for Older Homes in Aloha, OR

    Older homes, especially those built before modern waterproofing standards, are especially prone to water intrusion. Homes built from the late 1970s into the mid 1990s may still have polybutylene pipe, which is known for unexpected failures. Many older foundations may lack adequate exterior drainage or proper sealing. We specialize in assessing and upgrading waterproofing systems for these properties, often combining exterior excavation with interior drainage solutions. We respect the existing structure while installing modern, effective waterproofing, with attention to the challenges of older brick and stone foundations.
