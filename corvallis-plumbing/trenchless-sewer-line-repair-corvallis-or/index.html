    Pipe Bursting Sewer Repair in Corvallis

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method where a new pipe is pulled through the old one while the old pipe is fractured outward. This creates a new, seamless and often larger diameter sewer line without the need for long trenching. It is ideal for severely damaged or collapsed pipes in Corvallis that require full replacement rather than just relining.

    Causes of Sewer Line Damage in Corvallis, OR

    Sewer lines in Corvallis, OR can suffer damage from several factors, including shifting soils that cause pipes to crack or misalign. Hillside homes can face soil movement after heavy rain, which can shift buried water and sewer lines. Tree root intrusion is another major cause, as roots seek out moisture in pipes and grow, causing blockages and structural damage. Age and corrosion, especially in older cast iron or clay pipes, also add to deterioration. Our trenchless solutions address these common issues effectively.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Relining in Corvallis, OR

    CIPP relining creates a new pipe within an existing damaged sewer line. A resin-saturated felt tube is inserted into the old pipe and then inflated, curing in place to form a smooth, durable and watertight liner. This technique is well suited for repairing cracks, leaks and minor offsets without needing to dig up the entire line. It is a quick and effective solution for many common sewer problems faced by Oregon homeowners.
