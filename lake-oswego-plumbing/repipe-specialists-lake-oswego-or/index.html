    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Lake Oswego

    Slab leaks are a costly and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Hillside homes can face soil movement after heavy rain, which can crack buried water and sewer lines. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the compromised pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use advanced detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.

    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Lake Oswego

    PEX-A tubing is flexible, resists scale buildup and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water.
