    Control Valve and Injector Service in Portland, OR

    The control valve directs water through backwash, brine draw, rinse and refill. Tiny injectors and screens inside the valve clog with sediment and salt residue, which stops brine draw. We disassemble the injector, clean the screens and replace worn seals and spacers on valves like the Fleck 5600 and Clack WS1. A clean valve gives you a full regeneration every time.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Maintenance in Portland

    The cost of water softener maintenance in Portland, OR typically ranges from $100 to $250 for a standard service. This can vary based on the system's age, brand and any specific repairs needed. For a firm quote, please contact JFL Plumbing to assess your unit.

    A brine tank full of water usually means a clogged injector, a stuck float or a drain line problem. Our service clears the cause and resets the system.

    For the best performance and longevity, we recommend professional water softener maintenance once a year. In areas with extremely hard water, or for older systems, a twice-yearly check-up might be beneficial to prevent issues before they become serious.

    Usually, routine maintenance and minor repairs on an existing water softener do not require a plumbing permit in Portland. However, if major modifications or a full replacement of the system are needed, a permit might be required. We always ensure compliance with local codes.

    While you can perform basic tasks like checking salt levels and refilling the brine tank, professional maintenance involves inspecting internal components, testing resin efficiency and diagnosing control valve issues. These tasks require professional tools and experience to ensure the system functions correctly and safely.

    A salt bridge is a hard crust that forms in the brine tank, preventing salt from dissolving into the water. This means the system can't regenerate properly, leading to hard water. Our maintenance service includes breaking up and removing salt bridges, bringing back your softener's function.

    A standard water softener maintenance service typically takes about 1 to 2 hours. This can vary depending on the system's condition, age and whether any unexpected issues are discovered during the inspection.

    Resin often lasts 10 to 15 years, but chlorinated city water and iron can shorten that. Fine resin beads in your faucet aerators can signal resin breakdown.
