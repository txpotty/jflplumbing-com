    Clay and Orangeburg Line Replacement in Hillsboro, OR

    Trenchless Sewer Line Replacement in Hillsboro, OR

    To minimize disruption, trenchless sewer line replacement swaps out pipes without extensive digging. We offer pipe bursting, where a new pipe is pulled through the old one, fracturing the old pipe outward. This is ideal when protecting landscaping or driveways is a priority. The method works well for many homes in Hillsboro and can often be completed faster than traditional methods, using high-density polyethylene (HDPE) pipe known for its long life.

    Traditional Trench Sewer Line Replacement in Hillsboro

    For significant damage or certain pipe layouts, traditional trenching may be the most effective method. This involves digging a trench to access and replace the damaged sewer line directly.     Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Hillsboro

    Pipe bursting replaces the old sewer line by pulling a new pipe through the path of the old one. A bursting head breaks the old pipe outward while fused HDPE pipe follows close behind. This typically needs only an entry pit and an exit pit instead of a long trench. It works well under driveways, mature trees and landscaping. The new pipe is seamless, which leaves no seams for roots to enter.
