    Frequently Asked Questions About Rooter Services in Medford

    Rooting a sink, tub or toilet usually costs $125 to $300. Main sewer line rooting through a cleanout usually runs $200 to $500. Adding hydro jetting brings the total to about $450 to $1,000.

    Rooting cuts roots back but does not seal the pipe joints, so roots will eventually regrow. Root treatments slow regrowth, and pipe lining or replacement provides a permanent fix.

    Key signs include very slow drains, gurgling sounds coming from drains or toilets, foul odors from drains, and water backing up in sinks, tubs, or toilets. If multiple fixtures are draining slowly, it often points to a main sewer line blockage requiring professional rooter service.

    Our technicians take every precaution to keep your home clean during rooter service. We use drop cloths and protective gear to contain any mess, so the work area is clean after the job is done. We respect your property and aim for a clean, careful service.

    Rooting is safe when done by trained technicians using the correct cable and blade. Badly cracked or collapsed pipe can be harmed by any cleaning method. We watch for warning signs and stop if the pipe shows signs of damage.

    We generally advise against chemical drain cleaners, especially for stubborn clogs. These products can damage pipes over time, especially older ones common in Medford's older neighborhoods, and often only provide a temporary fix. Professional rooter services are safer and more effective for long-term solutions.

    Homes with tree roots or older clay pipe often need rooting every 12 to 24 months. Homes with newer PVC lines may go years without it. A camera inspection can help set the best schedule.

    Rental machines are available, but they can kink, grab and injure users who are not trained. They can also get stuck or damage older pipe. Professional rooter service is safer and usually more effective.
