    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Gresham, OR

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water and solids. Bellies usually form where the original bedding washed out or where the soil settled after years of wet weather. Clay soils are common in the western valleys of Oregon, and they stay wet for months, stressing sewer lines and foundations. We confirm the depth of standing water on camera, then re-bed and re-grade that section to restore proper fall. Correct slope is usually a quarter inch per foot for a typical residential line.
