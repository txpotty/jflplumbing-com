    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Grants Pass, OR

    For low-hazard applications, such as lawn irrigation systems in Grants Pass, OR, a pressure vacuum breaker, or PVB, is a popular and cost-effective backflow prevention device. PVBs protect against back-siphonage by letting air into the system when pressure drops. Our certified plumbers carefully install PVBs above the highest point of downstream piping, as code requires, to maintain effective protection. We also make sure of proper clearance and drainage.

    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Grants Pass

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines. They have two spring-loaded check valves and test cocks for annual certification. Unlike an RPZ, they do not discharge water, so they can be installed in vaults or below grade where local rules permit. We size them to avoid excessive pressure loss on your system.

    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Grants Pass

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of backflow protection and are often required for high-hazard applications in Grants Pass. These devices feature two independent check valves and a relief valve that opens to discharge water if pressure differentials indicate a backflow condition. JFL Plumbing specializes in precise RPZ installation, with proper sizing, placement and testing to meet all regulatory requirements for residential and commercial properties. We work with trusted brands like Watts and Febco.

    Reduced Pressure Zone Assembly Installation in Grants Pass, OR

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of protection and are required for high hazard connections like commercial kitchens, medical facilities, chemical injection and car washes. They include two independent check valves with a relief valve that dumps water if either check fails. Because the relief valve can discharge, we plan for drainage and install the assembly above grade where it can be tested easily. We carry trusted brands like Watts, Febco, Wilkins and Apollo.
