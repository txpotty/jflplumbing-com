    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Salem, OR

    Interior walls waterproofing focuses on managing water that has already passed through the foundation walls. This typically involves installing an interior drainage system, often called a French drain, along the perimeter of the basement floor. Water is collected and channeled to a sump pump, which then sends it safely away from the foundation. While not preventing water from entering the wall, it effectively stops it from pooling inside your living space. This solution is less disruptive than exterior excavation and works well where exterior access is limited.

    Foundation Crack Repair & Sealing in Salem

    Even minor cracks in foundation walls can allow serious water intrusion. Our services include crack repair using epoxy or polyurethane injection to seal these entry points for good. This method fills the entire depth of the crack, restoring strength and preventing water penetration. We address both vertical and horizontal cracks, knowing that horizontal cracks can signal structural issues that need a thorough assessment before sealing. Addressing these early prevents further water damage and expensive future repairs.

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Salem

    Exterior foundation waterproofing involves excavating around the foundation to apply a waterproof membrane or coating directly to the wall. This is often the most effective method for preventing water from entering the basement or crawl space. We use high-quality asphalt-modified polyurethane membranes or polymer-based coatings that resist hydrostatic pressure and soil movement. Clay soils are common in the western valleys of Oregon, and they stay wet for months, stressing sewer lines and foundations. Proper drainage solutions, such as French drains, are also integrated to divert water away.
