    Frequently Asked Questions About Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Lake Oswego

    Most tankless descaling visits cost $175 to $350, depending on brand and access. Adding an isolation valve kit typically runs $150 to $300 more.

    A standard tankless water heater maintenance service, including flushing and inspection, typically takes 1 to 2 hours. The exact time can vary depending on the unit's condition, the amount of scale buildup and any unexpected issues encountered during the service.

    Most visits take one to two hours, including the descaling cycle. Installing isolation valves on the same trip adds about an hour.

    Usually, a permit is not required for routine tankless water heater maintenance, such as flushing and inspections.     The descaling process involves shutting off the unit, isolating the water lines and connecting a circulating pump to flush a mild acidic solution through the heat exchanger. This solution dissolves mineral deposits like calcium and magnesium, which are common in hard water, restoring heat transfer efficiency.

    With regular descaling, many tankless heaters last 15 to 20 years. Neglected units in hard water can fail in under half that time.

    Neglecting maintenance can lead to several problems, including reduced heating efficiency, higher utility bills, decreased hot water output and eventually, costly breakdowns.
