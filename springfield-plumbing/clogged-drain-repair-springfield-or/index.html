    Bathroom Sink and Vanity Drain Clogs in Springfield, OR

    Bathroom sinks clog from hair, toothpaste and soap that bind together on the pop-up stopper and in the P-trap. Hard water can add a mineral crust that makes the pipe narrower over time. We pull and clean the stopper, clear the trap and run a small cable through the wall line. We can also replace worn pop-up assemblies and corroded tailpieces during the same visit.

    Tub and Shower Drain Clogs in Springfield

    Hair and soap scum are the most common causes of slow tubs and showers. We remove the strainer or overflow plate and pull the clog out with a drum auger designed for small diameter drains. If the drain is still slow, we check the trap under the slab or in the access panel. We take care not to scratch acrylic, fiberglass or porcelain finishes.

    Kitchen Sink Drain Clogs in Springfield

    Kitchen drains clog from cooking grease, coffee grounds and starchy food that stick to the pipe walls and harden. Garbage disposals can push food further down the line where it collects in horizontal runs. We remove the trap, run a cable through the trap arm and branch line and clear the buildup back to the main stack. For heavy grease, we may follow with a small jetter to wash the walls clear.

    Laundry and Utility Drain Clogs in Springfield, OR

    Washing machines discharge a lot of water quickly, and lint, fabric fibers and detergent residue build up in the standpipe and branch line. A clogged laundry drain often overflows at the standpipe mid-cycle. We clear the line with a cable and recommend a lint trap for the discharge hose. In some homes the laundry line ties into the kitchen line, so we check both.

    Outdoor Drain & Storm Drain Clearing in Springfield, OR

    Outdoor drains, including those for patios, driveways, and storm drains, are crucial for preventing water damage. Drought and water restrictions affect some areas in summer, so water-saving fixtures and irrigation upgrades are popular. These drains can become clogged with leaves, dirt, and debris. We provide complete cleaning and repair for outdoor drainage systems to ensure proper water runoff and protect your property's foundation in Springfield.
