    Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in Medford, OR

    The float switch is the most common reason a sump pump fails to start. Tethered floats can tangle on the pump body, and vertical floats can stick from mineral crust or debris. We clean or replace the switch and set the on and off levels so the pump does not short cycle. Electronic sensor switches are an option for tight pits where floats get hung up.

    Annual Sump Pump Check-ups in Medford

    Regular annual maintenance is key for the longevity and reliability of your sump pump. Our technicians perform a thorough inspection, checking the motor, impeller, float switch and discharge lines for any signs of wear or blockage. We also test the system's operation by filling the pit with water to ensure it activates correctly and pumps water away efficiently, protecting your Medford home from unexpected flooding.

    Emergency Sump Pump Diagnostics in Medford, OR

    If your sump pump is acting up, making unusual noises or not turning on, our emergency diagnostic service can quickly identify the problem. We use specialized tools to troubleshoot electrical issues, motor failures and float switch malfunctions. Prompt diagnosis and repair are essential to prevent water buildup, especially during unpredictable rainy seasons in Medford, OR.

    Severe storms often bring power outages along with the rain. A battery backup pump keeps water moving when the main pump loses power or fails.
