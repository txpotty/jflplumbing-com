    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Bend, OR

    Many homes built before the 1970s still have galvanized steel service lines that rust from the inside. Many rural homes rely on private wells, and owners around Bend benefit from testing for iron, hardness and other typical concerns. Hard water speeds up scale buildup, which restricts flow and causes rust-colored water. Patching galvanized pipe seldom holds up because the surrounding pipe is just as corroded. We usually recommend replacing the full run from meter to house with copper or PEX, which restores pressure and stops repeat leaks.

    Water Line Repair at the Meter in Bend, OR

    Leaks often start at the compression fittings and couplings right next to the meter box. Shifting soil and heavy traffic over the box can loosen those joints over time. We determine whether the leak is on the utility side or the customer side, since the water provider is in charge of the meter and the pipe before it. When the leak is yours, we replace the fittings and the customer shutoff valve on the same trip.

    Trenchless Water Line Repair in Bend

    For some water line repairs, traditional trenching can be avoided. Trenchless repair methods, like pipe bursting or pipe lining, allow us to fix or replace underground pipes with minimal disruption to your landscaping, driveway, or foundation. This method is often faster and less intrusive than traditional digging, making it a popular choice for homeowners in Bend looking to protect their property. We'll check whether your water line issue is a good candidate for trenchless technology.

    PEX Water Line Repair in Bend, OR

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping is a flexible, durable, and corrosion-resistant option more and more used in homes across Bend, OR. While PEX is less prone to leaks than older materials, damage can still occur from improper installation, rodent chewing, or impact. Our team is fully experienced in PEX repair, using specialized crimping tools and fittings to make strong, leak-proof connections. We make sure your PEX system continues to perform well.

    Main Water Line Replacement in Bend, OR

    When repairs are no longer cost-effective due to extensive damage or persistent issues, a full main water line replacement may be necessary. This often applies to older homes in Bend, OR with original galvanized steel or polybutylene pipes that are prone to corrosion and leaks. We offer full replacement services, upgrading your system with durable, modern materials like PEX or copper for a reliable water supply for decades to come. We handle all required permits and inspections.

    Galvanized Pipe Water Line Repair in Bend, OR

    Many older homes in Bend, OR still have galvanized steel water lines, which are prone to corrosion, rust, and reduced water flow over time. While replacement is often the best long-term solution, we can provide targeted repairs for specific leaks in galvanized pipes where feasible. Our plumbers are adept at working with these older systems, carefully assessing the pipe's condition and recommending the most effective repair strategy to extend its life or plan for eventual replacement.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Bend

    Many water line problems are made worse by high incoming pressure. A failed pressure reducing valve can push well over 80 psi into your plumbing, which stresses the service line and every fixture. We test static pressure with a gauge and replace worn regulators from brands like Watts and Zurn Wilkins. We also swap seized gate valves for full-port ball valves so you can shut the water off fast in an emergency.

    Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Bend

    Slab leaks, where water lines running beneath your home's concrete foundation leak, are a especially challenging issue. Oregon has a mix of older homes with galvanized supply pipe and cast iron drains alongside newer homes plumbed with PEX. These leaks can undermine your foundation, leading to major structural damage. We use specialized listening devices and camera inspections to pinpoint slab leaks precisely and offer repair options including direct slab penetration, re-routing, or pipe lining. Addressing a slab leak quickly in Bend is crucial to prevent extensive, costly foundation damage.

    Preventative measures include regular plumbing inspections, being mindful of tree roots near your water lines, and protecting outdoor pipes from extreme temperatures.
