    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Redmond

    CIPP lining is a popular trenchless method where we insert a resin-saturated felt tube into the existing damaged pipe. The liner is then cured in place using heat or air, creating a new, seamless pipe within the old one. This method is well suited for repairing cracks, breaks, and root intrusion without digging up your yard in Redmond.

    Pipe Bursting in Redmond, OR

    Pipe bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. A bursting head is pulled through the old pipe, fracturing it outward while a new HDPE pipe follows behind. The new pipe is fused into one continuous length with no joints. Bursting needs an entry and exit pit but avoids digging the full length.

    Commercial Trenchless Pipe Repair in Redmond

    Commercial properties often have sewer and storm lines under parking lots, sidewalks and buildings. Trenchless repair avoids costly concrete demolition and disruption to tenants and customers. We line larger diameter pipes and multiple laterals. We schedule work to minimize impact on business operations.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining in Redmond

    CIPP lining involves cleaning the old pipe, then inserting a flexible liner saturated with epoxy resin. The liner is inflated against the pipe wall and cured with hot water, steam or UV light. Once hardened, it forms a smooth, jointless pipe inside the old one. Lining works best for cracks, root intrusion and separated joints.
