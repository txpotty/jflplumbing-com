    Galvanized Pipe Repair in Hillsboro

    Galvanized steel pipe was common in older homes and rusts from the inside until flow drops and leaks start at threaded joints.     Drain and Waste Pipe Repair in Hillsboro

    Drain pipes made of ABS, PVC or cast iron can crack, separate or leak at joints. A leaking drain often shows up as a stain below a bathroom or a musty smell in a cabinet.     PEX Pipe Repair and Installation in Hillsboro

    PEX piping is a popular, flexible and freeze-resistant alternative to traditional copper or PVC, often used in newer construction and remodels in Hillsboro.     Pinhole Leak Repair in Hillsboro

    Pinhole leaks form when copper pipe wall thins from the inside, often due to water chemistry, high velocity or poor grounding. They start as tiny drips that stain drywall or ceilings. We cut out the affected section and replace it with new pipe or a PEX transition. If we find several pinholes in the same area, we will talk with you about repiping that section.
