    Pedestal Sump Pumps in Eugene

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor above the pit, which makes them easier to service and a good fit for narrow pits in older homes. They typically last a long time because the motor never sits in water. The tradeoff is more noise and a less finished look, so we generally recommend them for utility rooms rather than living spaces.
