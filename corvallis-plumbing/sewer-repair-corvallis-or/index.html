    Video Camera Sewer Inspection in Corvallis

    Before any repair, we use advanced video camera inspection to precisely locate and assess sewer line damage. This involves inserting a flexible rod with a camera into your sewer line, allowing us to see blockages, cracks, root intrusion or pipe collapses in real time. This no-dig method prevents unnecessary digging and helps us provide a precise diagnosis and repair plan. It's crucial for understanding issues in older homes in Corvallis and identifying problems like bellied pipes or collapsed sections.

    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Corvallis

    Hydro-jetting is a powerful cleaning method that uses high-pressure water streams to clear stubborn blockages, grease buildup and even tree roots from sewer lines. It's far more effective than snaking for thorough cleaning and preventing recurring clogs. This service is especially helpful for restaurants and commercial properties in Corvallis dealing with heavy grease accumulation, as well as homes with recurring drain issues.

    Sewer Line Backups and Blockages in Corvallis

    Sewer backups are usually caused by severe blockages from grease buildup, foreign objects or heavy tree root intrusion. Our team uses HD sewer camera inspections to identify the exact cause and location of the blockage. We then use hydro-jetting or mechanical snaking to clear the obstruction, restoring proper flow to your system and preventing further damage. This approach is very effective for the stubborn clogs often found in older Oregon homes.

    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Corvallis, OR

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water and solids. Bellies often form where the original bedding washed out or where the soil settled after heavy rain. Clay soils are common in the western valleys of Oregon, and they stay wet for months, stressing sewer lines and foundations. We confirm the depth of standing water on camera, then re-bed and re-grade that section to restore proper fall. Correct slope is typically a quarter inch per foot for a typical residential line.

    When trenchless repair isn't possible due to severe damage or specific pipe configurations, traditional dig-and-replace methods are necessary.     Sewer Vent Pipe Repair in Corvallis, OR

    While often overlooked, the sewer vent pipe is crucial for proper drainage and preventing sewer gas buildup. Issues with vent pipes, such as blockages from animal nests or damage from storms, can lead to slow drains, gurgling noises and foul odors.     Sewer Pipe Relining in Corvallis, OR

    Sewer pipe relining is a trenchless repair technique where a new, durable pipe is created within the existing damaged one. A flexible, epoxy-impregnated liner is inserted into the old pipe and inflated, curing in place to form a seamless, strong and corrosion-resistant new pipe. This method is effective for addressing cracks, minor breaks and root intrusion without extensive digging. It extends the life of your sewer system and is an great option for preserving landscapes in Corvallis.
