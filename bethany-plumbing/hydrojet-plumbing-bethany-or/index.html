    We utilize professional-grade hydrojetting equipment from reputable manufacturers like Spartan Tool, US Jetting, and Gorlitz. These brands are known for their reliability, power, and effectiveness in clearing even the toughest pipe blockages, ensuring a thorough clean every time.

    Repeat clogs, slow drains throughout the house, gurgling sounds and sewer odors all suggest buildup in the line. Kitchens that back up every few months are a telltale sign of grease. A camera inspection confirms whether jetting is the right fix.

    For most homes in Bethany, hydrojetting every 1 to 3 years can be helpful as preventive maintenance, especially if you have an older home, many mature trees, or frequently experience slow drains. Businesses with high-volume plumbing, like restaurants, may benefit from quarterly or semi-annual hydrojetting to prevent grease buildup.

    Rental jetters exist, but high pressure can injure you or damage a weak pipe if used incorrectly. Without a camera you also cannot tell whether the line is clean or broken. Professional jetting is safer and generally more effective.

    Jetting removes the roots inside the pipe, but roots will grow back if the joint or crack remains open. Regular jetting can control them for years. Pipe lining or repair is the permanent fix.

    While hydrojetting provides a thorough clean, some underlying issues like severely damaged or collapsed pipes might cause clogs to return.     When performed by a trained professional, hydrojetting is generally safe for most types of pipes, including older cast iron or PVC.
