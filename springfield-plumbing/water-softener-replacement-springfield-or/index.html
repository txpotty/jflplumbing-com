    To extend the life of your new water softener, use the correct type of salt, if applicable, and keep the brine tank filled. Regular maintenance, such as cleaning the brine tank and periodic inspections by a professional, can also help ensure its longevity and efficiency.

    In many areas, a plumbing permit is not usually required for a like-for-like water softener replacement, especially if there are no major changes to the existing plumbing lines.     With proper maintenance, most quality water softeners can last between 10 and 15 years, and sometimes even longer. Factors like water hardness, usage volume and the quality of the original installation can affect its lifespan. Regular servicing helps lengthen the life of your unit.

    Common signs include a return of hard water symptoms like scale buildup on fixtures, dry skin and hair, dingy laundry or heavy soap use. Other signs are a clear drop in water pressure, leaks from the unit, or a softener over 10 to 15 years old that needs frequent repairs.
