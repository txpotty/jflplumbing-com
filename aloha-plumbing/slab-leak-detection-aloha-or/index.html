    Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Aloha, OR

    Thermal imaging cameras allow us to see temperature differences on surfaces, which can indicate a hidden water leak. As water escapes a hot water line, it creates a distinct thermal signature on the concrete slab. This technique is particularly useful for hot water line leaks and helps us narrow down the search area significantly without any physical disruption to your property in Aloha.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Aloha, OR

    Cold water slab leaks do not create warm spots, so they are more difficult to see. They often show up as a running water meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards. We isolate the cold side and use acoustic equipment to listen for the leak through the slab. Line tracers map the pipe route so we know exactly where to listen.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Aloha

    Rerouting abandons the leaking line under the slab and runs new pipe through the walls or attic. It avoids breaking concrete and often costs less than direct access under finished floors. We usually use PEX for reroutes because it handles heat and resists hard-water corrosion. This option is a good fit when the slab is covered in tile or hardwood.

    Water and Sewer Line Slab Leaks in Aloha, OR

    Slab leaks can affect both freshwater supply lines and sewer drain pipes. Our detection services are equipped to identify both types of leaks, which present different challenges. Sewer line leaks often involve foul odors and slow drainage, while water line leaks can show up as wet spots or high water bills in your Aloha, OR home. We have specific methods for each.

    Pressure Testing & Isolation in Aloha

    When a leak is suspected, we can perform pressure tests on individual water lines. By isolating sections of your plumbing system and monitoring pressure gauges, we can confirm the presence of a leak and often determine which particular line is compromised. This systematic approach is crucial for homes with complex plumbing layouts, giving you an accurate diagnosis before any excavation in Aloha, OR.

    Repiping After Multiple Slab Leaks in Aloha, OR

    If your home has had two or more slab leaks, the remaining pipe is likely corroded too. Repiping replaces all supply lines with new PEX or copper run through the walls and attic. It is a bigger project, but it ends the cycle of recurring slab leaks. We can usually complete a full repipe in two to four days.
