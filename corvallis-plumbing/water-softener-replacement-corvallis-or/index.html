    Metered Softener Replacement in Corvallis

    Older timer-based softeners regenerate on a set schedule whether you used water or not. Metered softeners track actual usage and regenerate only when the resin is nearly used up. This saves salt, water and wear on the system. We replace old timer valves with metered Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1 valves. Most homeowners notice lower salt use within the first few weeks.

    Clack Water Softener Replacement in Corvallis

    Clack valves are a popular choice for their reliability and adjustable settings. If you have an older Clack system failing, JFL Plumbing offers expert Clack water softener replacement in Corvallis. We can replace just the control valve or the entire system, confirming compatibility with your existing setup and configuring the new unit for peak performance.

    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Corvallis, OR

    Softener resin can break down after 10 to 15 years, especially with chlorinated city water. When resin fails, you will see hard water symptoms even though the system is regenerating. Sometimes replacing resin and the tank is more cost effective than a whole new system. We inspect the valve and brine tank to decide. If the valve is old, a full replacement often makes more sense.

    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Corvallis

    Large households and homes with very hard water can benefit from a dual tank system that provides soft water all day and night. While one tank regenerates, the other stays in service. Kinetico and similar non-electric twin tank systems are popular for this reason. We size dual tank systems to your peak demand. They cost more upfront but prevent hard water bleed-through during regeneration.

    Commercial Softener Replacement in Corvallis

    Restaurants, salons and small businesses rely on soft water to protect dishwashers, espresso machines and water heaters. We replace commercial softeners sized to flow rate and daily usage. Larger valves and multiple tanks may be needed to meet demand. We schedule installs to limit downtime. We can also add filtration ahead of the softener if needed.

    Salt-Free Conditioner Alternatives in Corvallis, OR

    Some homeowners prefer a salt-free scale reduction system that uses template-assisted crystallization media. These systems do not remove hardness minerals but change them so they are less likely to stick. They require no drain and no salt. We explain the differences honestly, since they work differently than true softeners. They can be a good fit when drain access is limited or sodium is a concern.
