    Clogged Grease Trap Repair in Keizer

    Grease traps often clog due to excessive accumulation of fats, oils, and grease (FOGs) or food solids. Our technicians use high-pressure hydro-jetting to break down stubborn blockages and thoroughly clean the internal baffles and lines. We also inspect for underlying issues like damaged pipes or improper disposal practices that contribute to repeat clogs, offering solutions to prevent future blockages. Identifying the exact location and nature of the clog is crucial for effective repair and long-term performance in your Keizer establishment.

    Inlet and Outlet Tee Repair in Keizer, OR

    Tees direct flow into and out of the trap at the correct depth. Missing or broken tees allow floating grease to escape into the sewer line. We replace damaged tees with durable PVC or other approved materials. We also check that they are set at the right depth for your unit. This is one of the most common and important grease trap repairs.

    Commercial Grease Interceptor Repair in Keizer, OR

    Larger commercial establishments often utilize grease interceptors, which are more substantial than standard grease traps. These systems require specialized knowledge for repair, often involving more complex piping and larger capacity tanks. Our technicians are trained in the repair of all types and sizes of commercial grease interceptors, ensuring your high-volume kitchen in Keizer, OR remains compliant and functional, regardless of the system's complexity.

    Hydro-Jetting for Grease Trap Repair in Keizer

    When traditional snaking isn't enough, hydro-jetting is our go-to method for severe grease trap blockages. This powerful technique uses high-pressure water streams to fully scour the interior of pipes and the grease trap itself, removing years of FOG buildup, scale, and debris. It's a highly effective, non-invasive method that not only clears clogs but also cleans the lines, greatly reducing the likelihood of future blockages in your Keizer business.

    Baffle Repair and Replacement in Keizer

    Baffles slow incoming water so grease can rise and solids can settle. When baffles crack, corrode or break loose, grease passes straight through the trap. We remove damaged baffles and install new ones made for your trap model. In concrete interceptors, we can repair or rebuild baffle walls. Restoring baffles brings back proper separation and helps you stay up to code.
