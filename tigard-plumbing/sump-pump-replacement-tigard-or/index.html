    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Tigard, OR

    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to access for maintenance and often more durable. We provide professional pedestal sump pump replacement in Tigard, OR, with proper alignment and secure mounting. This type is often preferred in shallower pits or where noise is not a major concern, offering a robust solution for flood prevention.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Replacement in Tigard, OR

    Zoeller pumps are known for their reliability and heavy-duty construction, making them a top choice for many Tigard, OR homeowners. JFL Plumbing specializes in Zoeller sump pump replacement, so you get a high-quality, long-lasting unit. We can replace your existing Zoeller pump with a comparable model or upgrade you to a more powerful one to meet your needs and local conditions.

    Commercial Pit Pump Replacement in Tigard

    Commercial buildings often have elevator pits, parking garage drains and mechanical room sumps. These require pumps rated for higher flow and sometimes oil-sensing controls. We replace commercial pumps and alarms to meet code and insurance requirements. Duplex systems with alternating pumps provide a backup. We schedule work to avoid interrupting operations.

    Combination Sump Pump System Replacement in Tigard, OR

    For added protection, many homeowners in Tigard choose a combination sump pump system, which pairs a primary electric pump with a battery backup pump in one unit. This keeps the system running even if the power fails or the primary pump quits. JFL Plumbing can replace your existing combination system or install a new one, evaluating your needs for groundwater management. We make sure the components are well matched for strong performance and longevity.

    Yard Drainage Pit Pumps in Tigard, OR

    Some properties use pumps in yard drainage pits or below-grade window wells to handle storm runoff. These pumps may sit idle for months, then fail when they are needed most. We replace them with corrosion-resistant models and clear debris from the pit. We can route discharge away from foundations and onto approved areas. Regular testing before storm season is a smart habit.

    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Tigard

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves without binding. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats suit larger pits.

    High-Capacity Sump Pump Upgrades in Tigard

    If your current sump pump struggles to keep up with heavy water flow, especially during intense thunderstorms, it may be undersized. We offer high-capacity sump pump replacements and upgrades designed to handle large water volumes. Our plumbers will assess your water intrusion patterns and recommend a pump with greater horsepower and a higher GPM (gallons per minute) rating. This prevents the pump from cycling too frequently or failing under stress, common issues in some Tigard properties.

    Sump Pump Basin and Pit Replacement in Tigard, OR

    Sometimes it is not just the pump that needs replacing, since the sump basin or pit itself might be cracked, damaged or too small. JFL Plumbing offers sump pump basin and pit replacement in Tigard, OR so your entire system functions as it should.
