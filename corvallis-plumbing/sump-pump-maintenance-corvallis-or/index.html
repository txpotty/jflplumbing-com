    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Corvallis

    The discharge line carries water away from your basement, and the check valve prevents pumped water from flowing back into the pit. We inspect these components for blockages, leaks or damage. A faulty check valve can cause the pump to short-cycle, leading to early wear. We confirm these vital parts are functioning correctly to maintain an effective water removal system for your Corvallis residence.

    Annual Sump Pump Check-ups in Corvallis

    Regular annual maintenance is crucial for the longevity and reliability of your sump pump. Our technicians perform a thorough inspection, checking the motor, impeller, float switch and discharge lines for any signs of wear or blockage. We also test the system's operation by filling the pit with water to ensure it activates correctly and pumps water away efficiently, protecting your Corvallis home from unexpected flooding.

    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in Corvallis

    Drought and water restrictions affect some areas in summer, so efficient fixtures and irrigation upgrades are popular.     Maintenance Versus Replacement Cost in Corvallis, OR

    Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in Corvallis, OR

    The float switch is the most common reason a sump pump fails to start. Tethered floats can tangle on the pump body, and vertical floats can stick from mineral crust or debris. We clean or replace the switch and set the on and off levels so the pump does not short cycle. Electronic sensor switches are an option for tight pits where floats get hung up.

    Sump Pump Cleaning and Debris Removal in Corvallis

    Over time, sump pits can collect dirt, gravel and other debris, which can clog the pump's intake or jam the float switch. Our maintenance process includes carefully cleaning the sump pit and removing any obstructions. This lets the pump operate freely and efficiently, preventing breakdowns and extending its lifespan for your Corvallis property.
