    Frequently Asked Questions About Garbage Disposal Replacement in McMinnville

    The cost for garbage disposal replacement in McMinnville, OR typically ranges from $200 to $600. This includes the new unit itself, which can vary from $100 for a basic 1/3 HP model to over $300 for a high-end 1 HP unit, plus labor costs. Factors like the disposal's horsepower, brand, and any necessary plumbing modifications can influence the final price.

    If your current disposal jams often or struggles with food waste, a 3/4 or 1 HP model can make a real difference.

    Most replacements take about an hour. Upgrades with new electrical or drain changes may take a bit longer.

    We install InSinkErator, Waste King, Moen and GE. We help you pick based on power, noise and warranty.

    Manufacturer warranties typically range from two to ten years, and we guarantee our labor.

    For light use in a single-person household, 1/3 HP might suffice. Most homeowners opt for 1/2 HP or 3/4 HP for general use, which handles common food waste well. For large families, frequent cooking, or tougher waste like small bones, a 1 HP unit offers the best performance and durability.

    Common signs include frequent clogs, loud grinding noises without processing food, persistent leaks from the unit's housing, or if the disposal simply stops turning on. If you notice these issues, it's likely time for a replacement.

    A continuous feed disposal operates when you flip a wall switch, allowing you to add food waste while it's running.
