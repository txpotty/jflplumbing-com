    Pressure Relief Valve Testing in Bend

    A properly working temperature and pressure relief (T&P) valve is a crucial safety device for any hot water tank. Our technicians will test this valve to ensure it's not stuck or leaking, preventing dangerous pressure buildup within the tank. We verify it opens and closes correctly, and if it fails, we will suggest immediate replacement to keep your Bend home safe and compliant with plumbing codes.

    Burner and Pilot Light Maintenance in Bend, OR

    For gas hot water tanks, proper burner and pilot light operation is key for efficient heating. Our service includes cleaning the burner assembly to remove dust and debris that can impede combustion. We also check and adjust the pilot light, ensuring it ignites reliably. This attention to detail prevents common issues like intermittent hot water or complete heating failure, which can be frustrating for homeowners in Bend, OR.

    Attic and Garage Tank Checkups in Bend, OR

    Many homes put the water heater in a garage or attic where temperatures can swing widely. Heat speeds up the wear on plastic drain valves, gas valve seals and wiring insulation. We check drain pans, pan drain lines, platform height for gas units in garages and clearances around the tank. A leak in an attic tank without a working pan can destroy a ceiling, so we confirm that protection is in place.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Service in Bend, OR

This tank absorbs excess pressure created during the heating cycle, protecting your hot water tank and other plumbing fixtures. Our maintenance includes checking the expansion tank's air pressure and overall condition. Proper expansion tank function is key to preventing premature wear on your hot water tank and protecting system integrity.
