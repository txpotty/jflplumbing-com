    Electric tank water heaters are common in homes without natural gas service. We install models with the correct voltage and breaker size for your electrical panel. These units do not need venting, which allows more flexible placement. We also install a drain pan when the heater is in a closet or attic. Electric models are simple, dependable and quiet.

    Gas Water Heater Installation in Redmond, OR

    Gas water heaters are a popular choice in Redmond due to their efficiency and faster recovery rates.     Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Redmond

    The cost of water heater installation in Redmond, OR usually ranges from $1,200 to $3,500, depending on the type of water heater (tank vs. tankless), its capacity, fuel type (gas or electric) and any needed plumbing or electrical modifications. We provide a detailed, upfront quote after assessing your specific needs.

    New water heaters typically come with a manufacturer's warranty on the tank and components, usually ranging from 6 to 12 years. JFL Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation work, ensuring peace of mind. We will discuss exact warranty details for your chosen unit during the consultation.

    Most standard water heater installations, especially for replacing a similar tank unit, can be completed within 3 to 4 hours. However, a conversion from a tank to a tankless system, or installations requiring major plumbing or electrical modifications, might take a full day or longer. We will provide an estimated timeline during our initial assessment.

    While it might seem like a DIY project, water heater installation involves working with gas lines, high voltage electricity and water connections, all of which carry significant risks if not handled correctly. Improper installation can lead to leaks, gas explosions, electrical fires and carbon monoxide poisoning. It can also void your warranty, and local codes often require a licensed plumber for this work.

    A standard tank replacement usually takes two to four hours. Tankless installs or relocations can take most of a day.

    Common signs that you might need a new water heater include a lack of hot water, rusty or discolored water coming from the hot tap, banging or rumbling noises from the tank, visible leaks around the base of the unit and an age of 10 to 15 years or more. If you notice any of these issues, it is best to have a professional inspection.

    In most areas, a plumbing permit is required for water heater replacement or installation to ensure the work meets local building and safety codes. Permits are typically required for water heater replacements, sewer line work, new fixtures and remodels that move plumbing. JFL Plumbing handles all necessary permit applications and inspections in Redmond as part of our service.

    To improve efficiency, make sure your new water heater is properly sized for your home, consider a tankless model for on-demand heating, insulate hot water pipes and set the thermostat to 120°F (49°C) to prevent scalding and cut energy consumption.
