    Water Softener Replacement in Eugene, OR

    Older softeners can lose efficiency due to worn resin, failing valves or clogged injectors. We remove the old unit and install a new, more efficient model.     Water Hardness Testing and Programming in Eugene

    Accurate testing is essential for proper softener settings. We test your water for hardness, iron and chlorine before installation. Then we program regeneration frequency and salt dosage. This helps reduce salt use while keeping water soft. We review settings with you so you understand how the system works.

    Softener Loop Installation in Eugene

    A softener loop is the plumbing connection that routes your main water line through a softener. Many newer homes include a loop, but older homes often do not. We cut into the main line, install the loop and add a bypass valve. We keep outdoor hose bibs and irrigation on hard water when possible to save salt. A proper loop makes future service easy.

    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Eugene

    Reverse osmosis systems remove dissolved solids, which can be high in some water supplies. We install RO units under the kitchen sink with a dedicated faucet. Some systems also connect to refrigerators or ice makers. RO pairs well with a softener for complete water treatment. We change filters on a schedule so the system keeps working well.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Installation in Eugene

    The cost of water softener installation in Eugene, OR can range from $1,500 to $4,000, depending on the type and size of the system, your home's existing plumbing and any needed modifications. This typically includes the unit itself and the labor for professional installation. We provide detailed, upfront pricing after an on-site assessment.

    We offer a warranty on our workmanship, and the water softener units themselves come with manufacturer warranties, which typically range from 5 to 10 years for the tank and control valve. We will provide you with all warranty details for your specific system after installation.

    A properly sized and installed water softener should not significantly affect your water pressure. If you experience a noticeable drop in pressure after installation, it could point to an issue with the system or installation, which our plumbers can diagnose and resolve.

    A water softener specifically targets hard minerals like calcium and magnesium to prevent scale buildup. A water filter, on the other hand, is designed to remove impurities like chlorine, sediment and other contaminants to improve water taste and safety. Many homeowners in Eugene choose to install both for comprehensive water treatment.

    Many homes benefit from a softener when the water is hard. Soft water can be slightly corrosive, which may lead to pinhole leaks in copper pipe and blue-green stains on fixtures. Softeners protect water heaters, fixtures and appliances from scale.

    Softeners remove hardness minerals using salt, while conditioners change how minerals behave to reduce scale. Softeners are more reliable for very hard water.

    In many areas, a plumbing permit is required for major plumbing alterations like water softener installation to confirm compliance with local codes. JFL Plumbing will advise you on any required permits for your Eugene installation and can assist with the application process.
