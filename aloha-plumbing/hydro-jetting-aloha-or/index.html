    Frequently Asked Questions About Hydro Jetting in Aloha

    While hydro jetting is very powerful, it is generally safe for most sound plumbing systems, including PVC, cast iron, and concrete pipes.     No permit is needed for jetting. If the inspection shows damage that needs repair, permits may be required and we handle them.

    For most homes in Aloha, hydro jetting every 1 to 3 years can be a good preventative measure, especially if you have recurring drain issues or older pipes. Businesses, particularly restaurants, may benefit from quarterly or twice-yearly hydro jetting to manage grease buildup. We can suggest a schedule based on your needs.

    For most modern and well-maintained pipes, hydro jetting is very safe. However, for very old, corroded, or severely damaged pipes, the high pressure could cause further damage.
