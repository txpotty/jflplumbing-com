    Sewer Belly and Sagging Pipe Clogs in Springfield, OR

    Clay soils are common in the western valleys of Oregon, and they stay wet for months, stressing sewer lines and foundations. Our camera and locator show where the belly is and how deep it sits. We can then quote a spot repair to restore proper slope.
