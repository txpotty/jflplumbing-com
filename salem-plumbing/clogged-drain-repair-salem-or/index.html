    Recurring Drain Clogs and Camera Diagnosis in Salem, OR

    If the same drain clogs every few weeks, the problem is often a sagging pipe, a broken fitting or heavy scale. We run a small inspection camera through the line to see the inside of the pipe.     Main Sewer Line Clog Clearing in Salem, OR

    A clogged main sewer line can affect all drains in your home and is a major issue. Causes often include tree root intrusion, pipe collapse, or significant grease buildup, common in older homes.     Laundry and Utility Drain Clogs in Salem, OR

    Washing machines discharge a lot of water quickly, and lint, fabric fibers and detergent residue build up in the standpipe and branch line. A clogged laundry drain often overflows at the standpipe mid-cycle. We clear the line with a cable and recommend a lint trap for the discharge hose. In some homes the laundry line ties into the kitchen line, so we check both.

    Hydro-Jetting Services in Salem, OR

    For severe or recurring clogs, hydro-jetting is an excellent solution. This method uses high-pressure water streams to blast away buildup, grease, scale, and even tree roots from your pipes. It's a thorough cleaning process that leaves pipes scoured, reducing the likelihood of future blockages and extending pipe life, especially in homes with hard water deposits.
