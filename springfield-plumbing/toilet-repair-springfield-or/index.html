    Weak Flush and Clog Repair in Springfield, OR

    If your toilet needs two flushes or clogs often, the problem could be scale in the rim jets, a partial blockage in the trapway or a flush valve that does not open fully. We auger the trap, check the vent if gurgling is present and clean the jets with a descaling treatment. Some low-flow toilets from the 1990s simply flush poorly, and we will tell you plainly if replacement is the better fix.

    Pressure-Assist and Dual-Flush Repair in Springfield

    Pressure-assist toilets from Sloan Flushmate and dual-flush models from TOTO and American Standard use parts that hardware stores seldom stock. We carry cartridges, flush buttons and seals for the common models and can order specialty parts fast. These systems need correct pressure settings to work well, so we test incoming water pressure as part of the repair.

    Toilet Tank and Bowl Crack Repair in Springfield

    Cracks in a toilet tank or bowl in Springfield can lead to serious water leakage and require immediate attention. While small hairline cracks might be temporarily sealed, larger or structural cracks often necessitate replacement of the affected component. Our technicians assess the severity of the crack and advise on the most cost-effective and durable solution. We can perform careful tank or bowl replacements, ensuring a watertight seal and proper function, preventing further water damage to your flooring or subfloor.

    Toilet Gasket and Seal Replacement in Springfield, OR

    The gaskets and seals within your toilet tank and at its base are essential for preventing leaks. Over time, these rubber components can degrade, crack, or become brittle due to age and exposure to water, especially where the water is hard. We specialize in replacing worn-out flapper seals, tank-to-bowl gaskets, and the wax ring at the toilet's base, which is essential for preventing leaks onto your bathroom floor and maintaining a watertight seal.

    Fill Valve Replacement in Springfield

    A noisy, slow or hissing tank often points to a worn fill valve. Mineral scale clogs the small ports inside, which causes long refill times and phantom refills in the night. We replace old ballcock valves with modern adjustable fill valves from Fluidmaster or Korky and set the water level to the manufacturer mark. The job takes about 20 minutes and makes an older toilet noticeably quieter.

    Running Toilet Repair in Springfield

    A running toilet is not just annoying, it can waste hundreds of gallons of water a day, leading to much higher water bills.
