    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Oregon City, OR

    PEX-A, such as Uponor, uses an expansion tool to stretch the pipe and a ring over the fitting, which then shrinks back into a tight seal. The fittings have a larger inner diameter, so flow restriction is lower. PEX-A is also more flexible and can be repaired from kinks with a heat gun. It is our favored choice for many full repipes.

    Whole-Home PEX Repiping in Oregon City

    If your home has old, corroded or leaking pipes made of galvanized steel or polybutylene, a whole-home PEX repipe is an excellent solution. We remove the outdated piping and install a complete PEX system, improving water pressure and water quality and stopping costly leaks. This comprehensive service leaves your entire plumbing system modern, efficient and built to last for decades in your Oregon City home.

    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Oregon City

    We offer installation for both PEX-A and PEX-B piping and understand the distinct advantages of each. PEX-A, often preferred for its flexibility and ability to recover from minor kinks, uses expansion fittings. PEX-B is more rigid but also very durable, usually using crimp or compression fittings. Our plumbers in Oregon City can help you decide which type of PEX suits your project, considering factors like budget and application.

    PEX for Water Line Replacement in Oregon City

    When your main water line from the street to your home needs replacement, PEX is an smart option. Its flexibility allows for easier trenchless or minimal-dig installations, limiting disruption to your yard in Oregon City. PEX main lines handle ground movement and resist corrosion better than rigid pipe. Crawlspace foundations are very common here, which makes many plumbing repairs more accessible than under a slab.

    Hydronic Heating with PEX in Oregon City

    PEX is also a leading choice for hydronic heating systems, including radiant floor heating. Its ability to withstand high temperatures and resist corrosion makes it well suited for circulating hot water. If you are considering radiant heat for a specific area of your home or business in Oregon City, PEX is the pipe to use.

    Uponor PEX System Installation in Oregon City

    JFL Plumbing frequently installs Uponor PEX systems, a leading brand known for high-quality, durable PEX-A piping.     Full Home PEX Repiping in Oregon City

    If your home has old, corroded or leaking pipes, a full home PEX repipe can greatly improve your plumbing system. We remove outdated galvanized, copper or polybutylene pipes and replace them with durable PEX, upgrading your entire water delivery system. This prevents future leaks, improves water pressure and ends the rust-colored water often seen with older piping in Oregon City.

    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in Oregon City, OR

    A manifold system sends a dedicated line from a central hub to each fixture, much like a breaker panel for water. Each line gets its own shutoff, which makes repairs easier and keeps pressure steady when multiple fixtures run. Home-run layouts use more pipe but fewer fittings in walls. We often combine manifold and trunk-and-branch designs to balance cost and performance.
