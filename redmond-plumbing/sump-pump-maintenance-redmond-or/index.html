    Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in Redmond, OR

    The float switch is the most frequent reason a sump pump fails to start. Tethered floats can tangle on the pump body, and vertical floats can stick from mineral crust or debris. We clean or replace the switch and set the on and off levels so the pump does not short cycle. Electronic sensor switches are an option for tight pits where floats get hung up.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Maintenance in Redmond

    JFL Plumbing is proficient in servicing Zoeller sump pumps, a widely trusted brand known for its durability. Our maintenance includes specific checks for Zoeller models, such as inspecting the cast iron housing for cracks, ensuring the motor is running smoothly and testing the float switch assembly for correct activation. We stock common replacement parts for quick repairs, keeping your Zoeller pump in peak condition.

    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in Redmond

    Heavy, prolonged rain is the main storm concern in western Oregon, saturating soil and pushing water into crawlspaces and basements.     Pedestal Sump Pump Check-Ups in Redmond, OR

    For homes equipped with pedestal sump pumps, JFL Plumbing provides dedicated maintenance services in Redmond, OR.     Annual Sump Pump Check-ups in Redmond

    Regular annual maintenance is key for the longevity and reliability of your sump pump.     Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Redmond

    The discharge line carries water away from your basement, and the check valve prevents pumped water from flowing back into the pit. We inspect these components for blockages, leaks or damage. A faulty check valve can cause the pump to short-cycle, leading to premature wear.
