    Pipe Bursting Services in Eugene, OR

    For severely damaged or collapsed pipes, pipe bursting offers a full replacement solution. A new pipe is pulled through the old one, breaking apart the old pipe at the same time. This provides a completely new, durable pipe, often HDPE (high-density polyethylene), which resists corrosion and leaks. It's an effective long-term fix for extensive issues in Eugene, OR.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Eugene

    CIPP lining is a proven trenchless method where we insert a resin-saturated felt tube into the existing damaged pipe. The liner is then cured in place using heat or air, creating a new, seamless pipe within the old one. This method is well suited for repairing cracks, breaks, and root intrusion without digging up your yard in Eugene.

    Trenchless vs Traditional Repair Cost in Eugene, OR

    Trenchless repair can cost more per foot of pipe than open trenching. However, once you add the cost of restoring driveways, patios, landscaping and irrigation, trenchless is often the better value. It is also faster and less disruptive. We compare both methods with real numbers for your property.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining in Eugene

    CIPP lining involves cleaning the old pipe, then inserting a flexible liner saturated with epoxy resin. The liner is inflated against the pipe wall and cured with hot water, steam or UV light. Once hardened, it forms a smooth, jointless pipe inside the old one. Lining works best for cracks, root intrusion and separated joints.
