    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Hillsboro

    CIPP lining is a proven trenchless method where we insert a resin-saturated felt tube into the existing damaged pipe. The liner is then cured in place using heat or air, creating a new, seamless pipe within the old one. This method is excellent for repairing cracks, breaks, and root intrusion without digging up your yard in Hillsboro.

    Trenchless vs Traditional Repair Cost in Hillsboro, OR

    Trenchless repair can cost more per foot of pipe than open trenching. We compare both methods with real numbers for your property.

    Pipe Bursting in Hillsboro, OR

    Pipe bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. A bursting head is pulled through the old pipe, fracturing it outward while a new HDPE pipe follows behind. The new pipe is fused into one continuous length with no joints. Bursting needs an entry and exit pit but avoids digging the full length.
