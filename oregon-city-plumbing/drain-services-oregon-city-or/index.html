    Hydro Jetting in Oregon City

    Hydro jetting sends water at up to 4,000 psi through a specialized nozzle that scours the full inside wall of the pipe. We camera the line first to make sure the pipe is sound enough for jetting. Most residential jetting jobs take about two to three hours.
