    Brine Tank Cleaning and Salt Bridge Removal in Bend, OR

    The brine tank is crucial for salt dissolution and regeneration. Over time, salt residue and other impurities can accumulate, forming a 'salt bridge' that prevents the system from properly regenerating. Our maintenance service includes thorough cleaning of the brine tank and removal of any salt bridges, so your system can draw brine effectively for full softening. This is a common issue we address in Bend homes.

    Routine Water Softener Inspections in Bend

    Regular inspections are the cornerstone of effective water softener maintenance. This preventive approach helps extend the life of your unit and keeps performance consistent, especially since water hardness varies from place to place.

    Resin Bed Cleaning and Regeneration Optimization in Bend, OR

    The resin beads are responsible for ion exchange, removing hard minerals from your water. Over time, these beads can become fouled with iron or other contaminants, reducing their capacity. Our maintenance includes cleaning the resin bed and adjusting the regeneration cycle settings for full softening capacity. We confirm your system is regenerating efficiently for your water quality, providing truly soft water.

    Bypass Valve and Drain Line Repair in Bend, OR

    A leaking bypass valve can let hard water slip past the softener without you noticing. We test and rebuild bypass valves and replace cracked fittings. We also inspect the drain line for kinks and proper air gap and the brine overflow line for correct routing. These small fixes head off leaks and backflow problems.
