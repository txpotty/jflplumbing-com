    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacement in Keizer

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method used for full sewer line replacement. It involves pulling a new pipe through the old one while fracturing the existing pipe. This creates a seamless, durable replacement, often using high-density polyethylene pipe, which is highly resistant to corrosion and root intrusion, common problems in older Keizer homes. This method is well suited when the existing pipe is severely damaged or collapsed.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Keizer, OR

    Tree and shrub roots seek out moisture in sewer joints. Once inside, they grow into dense masses that cause recurring backups. We cut roots with a mechanical cutter or hydro jet, then line the pipe to seal the joints. That breaks the cycle of cleaning the line every couple of months.

    Sectional Point Repair in Keizer

    When only one section of a line is damaged, a point repair can fix it without lining the whole length. We position a short resin liner at the damaged spot using the camera and inflate it until it cures. Point repairs are a cost-effective fix for a single crack, hole or root entry. They are common on otherwise healthy lines.

    Orangeburg and Clay Pipe Repair in Keizer

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg fiber pipe or vitrified clay sewer laterals. Orangeburg flattens and blisters over time, while clay cracks and separates at joints. Pipe bursting is often the best option for Orangeburg, and lining can work well for clay that still holds its shape. We determine the material on camera before recommending a method.
