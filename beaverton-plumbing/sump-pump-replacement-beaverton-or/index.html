    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Beaverton

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves freely. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats work best in larger pits.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Replacement in Beaverton

    A standard sump pump replacement usually costs $500 to $1,200 installed. Battery backup systems usually add $800 to $1,800. Sewage ejector pumps generally cost $1,200 to $2,500.

    A battery backup sump pump system is a secondary pump powered by a battery, designed to turn on automatically if your primary sump pump loses power during an outage or fails. This is critical for maintaining flood protection during power outages caused by severe weather.

    Constant running, loud grinding, frequent cycling or failure to start are typical signs. Rust, age and a burning smell also indicate trouble.

    Most standard sump pump replacements can be finished within 2 to 4 hours. More complex installations, such as those requiring new pit excavation or extensive discharge line work, might take longer, up to a full day.

    We install a variety of trusted sump pump brands known for their reliability and performance, including Zoeller, Wayne, Liberty Pumps and Little Giant. Our plumbers can help you choose the best brand and model for your home's requirements and budget.

    To maintain your new sump pump, regularly check the pit for debris, test the pump monthly by pouring water into the pit, and make sure the discharge pipe is clear. An annual professional inspection by JFL Plumbing can also help extend its lifespan.

    While it may seem like a simple task, sump pump replacement involves electrical connections, proper plumbing and making sure the pump is correctly sized and installed to prevent future flooding. DIY replacement can lead to improper operation, electrical hazards or system failure. It is best to hire a professional plumber for a safe, proper and effective installation.
