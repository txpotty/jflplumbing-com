    Slab Leak Detection in Bend

    We start by shutting off fixtures and watching the meter to verify a leak. Then we isolate the hot and cold lines to find out which one is leaking. Using acoustic ground microphones, we listen for the sound of escaping water through the concrete. Thermal imaging shows hot water leaks as warm areas on the floor surface.

    Pipe Rerouting and Repiping in Bend

    For more extensive damage, multiple leaks, or pipes that are too old and corroded to repair reliably, pipe rerouting is a long-term solution. This involves abandoning the leaking line under the slab and installing a new pipe through the walls or attic. This method bypasses the problem area entirely, providing a fresh, durable plumbing line. It is a common solution for aging homes in Bend with original copper or galvanized plumbing.

    PEX Repiping for Slab Leaks in Bend, OR

    If multiple slab leaks indicate a systemic issue with your home's original plumbing, a full PEX repipe might be the most cost-effective and durable solution. PEX tubing is flexible, corrosion-resistant, and less prone to leaks caused by foundation shifts, making it a good choice for many homes. We can replace all the water supply lines in your Bend home, providing a brand-new, leak-free plumbing system that will last for decades.

    Slab Leak Repair for PEX and Copper Lines in Bend, OR

    Whether your home in Bend, OR has modern PEX piping or traditional copper lines, our plumbers are skilled in repairing both.
