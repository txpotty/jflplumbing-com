    Frequently Asked Questions About PEX Pipe Installation in Bend

    A whole-home PEX repipe typically costs $4,500 to $12,000 depending on home size, bathroom count and access. Smaller jobs like a single bathroom remodel rough-in often run $800 to $2,500.

    Properly installed PEX is expected to last several decades. Manufacturers often offer 25 year warranties when their pipe and fittings are used together.

    PEX offers several advantages over copper, including lower material and installation costs, greater flexibility and resistance to corrosion and freezing. Unlike copper, PEX does not develop pinhole leaks over time due to water chemistry, making it a more durable choice for many Bend homes. It also cuts down on water hammer noise.

    Signs you might need PEX repiping include persistently low water pressure, discolored or rusty water, frequent leaks, or old galvanized steel or polybutylene pipes. These issues are typical in older Bend homes and point to a failing plumbing system.

    Multiple pinhole leaks, discolored water, low pressure throughout the house and gray polybutylene pipe are common signs. Recurring slab leaks are also a strong reason to repipe overhead.

    PEX can be used for outdoor applications like irrigation lines or outdoor spigots, but it needs to be protected from direct UV light, which can break down the material over time. When used outdoors, PEX should consistently be buried or otherwise shielded.

    PEX is a good fit for a wide range of climates because of its flexibility and resistance to freezing. The eastern side of the state is drier, with hotter summers and colder winters that strain pipes and water heaters differently. While rigid pipes can burst when water freezes, PEX can expand and contract, significantly reducing the risk of burst pipes during cold snaps. It also performs well in high temperatures, making it a reliable choice year-round in Bend.

    We mainly use high-quality, reputable PEX brands such as Uponor (PEX-A) and Viega (PEX-B), which are known for their durability and reliability. These brands meet or exceed industry standards and are approved for use in Oregon plumbing applications, providing a long-lasting and dependable plumbing system for your home.
