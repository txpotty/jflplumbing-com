    Dry Well Cleaning and Inspection in Lake Oswego, OR

    Dry wells let storm water soak into the ground instead of flowing to a street. They are found on commercial sites, HOA common areas and some residential lots. Silt slowly seals the settling chamber and the drainage rock, slowing how fast water drains. We vacuum the chamber, inspect the inlet and outlet, and check drain-down time. If the dry well no longer drains properly, we explain options for repair or replacement.

    Yard and Area Drain Cleaning in Lake Oswego

    Yard drains and area inlets collect runoff from lawns, rock landscaping and patios. Over time the grates fill with leaves and the pipes fill with silt and small gravel. We pull the grates, vacuum the inlet box and jet the pipe toward its outlet. This restores the line so it can carry a full storm. We also check that grates are secure and seated.

    Channel and Trench Drain Cleaning in Lake Oswego

    Channel drains across driveways and garage entries catch water before it reaches the house. Their narrow channels fill quickly with sand, leaves and debris. We remove the grates, clean the channel and jet the outlet pipe. Cracked or loose grates can be replaced while we are there. A clean trench drain keeps water out of your garage during heavy rain.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Storm Drain Cleaning in Lake Oswego

    The cost of storm drain cleaning in Lake Oswego usually ranges from $200 to $600, depending on the severity and type of blockage, the length of the drain, and the equipment required, such as hydro jetting or snaking. More complex issues like extensive root intrusion or collapsed pipes may cost more. We provide a written, upfront estimate before starting any work.

    Cleaning does not need a permit. New dry wells or major drainage changes may require permits or registration, and we can guide you.

    Hydro jetting is a powerful drain cleaning method that uses high-pressure water streams to wash out stubborn blockages and thoroughly clean the interior of drain pipes. For storm drains, it is highly effective at removing compacted soil, heavy silt, grease, and roots, restoring the drain to its full flow capacity much better than snaking.

    It is generally recommended to have your storm drains inspected and cleaned at least once a year. Properties with many trees or those that see frequent heavy rains may benefit from twice-yearly cleaning to prevent buildup and blockages.

    Our technicians are trained to service all types and brands of storm drain systems commonly found in residential and commercial properties. This includes standard PVC, corrugated HDPE, concrete, and cast iron drainpipes, as well as various types of catch basins and grates from manufacturers like NDS, ADS, and Zurn.

    Common signs include water pooling around your property after rain, slow drainage from downspouts, visible debris piling up at catch basin grates, and unusual odors coming from the drains. If you notice any of these, it is a good idea to have your storm drains checked.

    We recommend having your storm drains inspected and possibly cleaned annually, especially if you have mature trees nearby. Storm drains and sewer lines can be overloaded during intense rain events, leading to backups in low-lying homes. Properties prone to debris buildup or with a history of clogs may benefit from twice-yearly cleaning to prevent major issues.

    In Lake Oswego, common causes of storm drain clogs include built-up leaves, dirt, and organic debris from trees, silt and sediment from soil erosion, trash, and unfortunately, sometimes even illegally dumped waste.
