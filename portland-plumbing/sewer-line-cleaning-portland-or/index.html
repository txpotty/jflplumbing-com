    Identifying Signs of Sewer Line Clogs in Portland, OR

    Knowing the signs of a potential sewer line clog can help you act fast. Watch for multiple drains backing up at once, gurgling noises from toilets or drains, a sewage smell in your yard or basement, or water coming up through a drain when you flush a toilet in Portland, OR. If you notice any of these symptoms, it is time to call JFL Plumbing for a professional sewer line inspection and cleaning.

    Sewer Line Camera Inspection in Portland

    A camera inspection shows the inside of your sewer line in real time. We look for bellies where water pools, offset joints, cracks, roots and corrosion. This technique is particularly useful for thoroughly cleaning pipes without causing damage, making it suitable for a wide range of materials from PVC to older clay pipes found in Portland, OR. It leaves your sewer lines virtually spotless, greatly reducing the chance of future blockages.

    Root-Infested Sewer Line Cleaning in Portland, OR

    Roots are the top cause of sewer line blockages in established neighborhoods. They enter at clay joints and small cracks, then grow into a dense mat that catches waste. We cut them out with root saws and finish with jetting to remove the fine hairs. The camera then shows each entry point. You can choose ongoing cleaning or a permanent fix such as lining.

The goal is a line that flows at full capacity. Most homeowners with older pipe do well with cleaning every one to two years.
