    Frequently Asked Questions About Clogged Drain Repair in Beaverton

    Clearing a single sink, tub or shower drain usually costs $125 to $300. Harder clogs that need a larger machine or access through a roof vent run $250 to $450. Hydro jetting a heavily scaled or greasy line usually costs $350 to $800.

    Hydro-jetting uses high-pressure water to clear stubborn blockages and completely clean the inside of your pipes. It's often necessary for severe clogs caused by tree roots, heavy grease buildup, or significant mineral deposits from hard water, which traditional snaking might not fully clear.

    We use professional equipment from trusted brands like Spartan Tool, RIDGID, and Gorlitz. These tools are known for their reliability and effectiveness in diagnosing and clearing various types of drain clogs, supporting a high standard of service.

    To prevent future clogs, avoid pouring grease down kitchen drains, use drain screens to catch hair and food particles, and flush only toilet paper. Regular professional drain maintenance, especially in areas with hard water, can also help limit mineral buildup.

    Most single fixture clogs are cleared in 30 to 90 minutes. Lines with heavy buildup or limited access can take longer. We tell you if the job will run beyond the usual time.

    Most common household clogs, like those in sinks or showers, can be cleared within one to two hours. More severe blockages, such as main sewer line clogs requiring camera inspection and hydro-jetting, might take a few hours to a full day, depending on the complexity.

    Common signs of a clogged drain include slow drainage, gurgling sounds from drains or toilets, unpleasant odors coming from your pipes, and water backing up into sinks, tubs, or showers. If multiple drains are affected, it often indicates a main line issue.
