    Like-for-Like Tankless Swaps in Corvallis

    When your existing tankless has a cracked heat exchanger or a failed control board that is not worth repairing, a direct replacement is often the quickest fix. We match the new unit to your current gas line, venting type and mounting location whenever possible. Many Rinnai and Navien models share similar footprints, which keeps wall patching to a minimum. We also replace the old service valves if they are corroded or seized. Most like-for-like swaps are finished in four to six hours.

    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Corvallis, OR

    Converting from a 40 or 50 gallon tank to tankless frees up floor space and ends standby heat loss in a garage or closet. The catch is that tankless units usually need a larger gas line, often three quarter inch or bigger, plus new venting. We check your meter size and pipe run before quoting so there are no surprises mid-job. We also cap or reroute the old flue and seal the roof penetration if it is no longer used. Plan for a conversion to take a full day.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Corvallis

    Many homes in Corvallis rely on natural gas for water heating, and gas tankless units are a highly efficient option. Our gas tankless water heater replacement service includes thorough inspection of your existing gas lines, proper sizing of new gas lines if needed, and secure venting to the outdoors. We prioritize safety. All gas connections and combustion air requirements meet Oregon and local codes, preventing potential hazards and maximizing efficiency.

    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Corvallis

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE series or Rinnai Sensei reach efficiencies above 90 percent by recovering heat from exhaust gases. They vent through inexpensive PVC and produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized and drained properly. We install a neutralizer cartridge and route the condensate line to an approved drain. Damp crawlspaces are common, and constant moisture can corrode exposed metal pipe and fittings over time. Condensing units still save gas because they work year-round on showers, laundry and dishes. We explain the cost difference so you can decide if the efficiency gain is worth it.

    Tankless vs. Tank Water Heater Conversion in Corvallis, OR

    Thinking about converting from a traditional tank water heater to a tankless system in your Corvallis, OR home? This is a common and worthwhile upgrade, but it requires specialized expertise. We handle the entire conversion, which can involve rerouting gas lines, upgrading electrical service, modifying water lines and installing new venting. Our team ensures a smooth transition, so you can enjoy the long-term benefits of a modern, efficient tankless system.

    Recirculation Tankless Upgrades in Corvallis, OR

    If you are tired of running the tap while you wait for hot water, a tankless with a built-in recirculation pump can bring heat to the fixture in seconds. Navien A series and Rinnai units with internal pumps can use a dedicated return line or a crossover valve under the farthest sink. We program schedules so the pump runs only during your morning and evening peaks. That keeps gas use in check while saving thousands of gallons of wasted water a year. This upgrade is popular in long single-story ranch homes.
